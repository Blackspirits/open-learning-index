# Event-driven re-verification — Iniciação à Língua Portuguesa archive status

Date: **2026-09-14**

Course: `nau-iniciacao-lingua-portuguesa`

## Trigger

A learner-facing edX course page showed **“This course is archived”**. The canonical publication record still said `status=active`, so the discrepancy triggered the immediate maintenance review required by `docs/maintenance.md`.

## Primary-source findings

- NAU's current Universidade do Porto provider catalogue lists **Iniciação à Língua Portuguesa** under **Outras edições** with status **Arquivado**.
- The NAU course page still documents the original 32-hour, eight-unit European-Portuguese curriculum and its historical assessment design.
- The canonical edX URL remains reachable as the migrated PortugalX route.
- Current edX help states that archived courses can retain most lecture/reading material in free audit access, but graded assignments stop updating, course staff are inactive, Verified upgrade/certification is unavailable, and some assignments or links may no longer work.

## Decision

The resource remains publication-eligible as **F2_CONTENT_ONLY + active_archive** rather than being removed:

- substantial teaching content remains available;
- the archive does **not** provide a current graded/certificate path;
- European-Portuguese beginner coverage remains a real canonical gap;
- archive status materially weakens currentness, practice reliability and accessibility.

The Deep Review is superseded rather than silently rewritten. Quality moves from **8.61 → 8.38** and Recommendation from **9.0 → 8.6**. The Phase-4 admission is likewise superseded and still resolves to **admit**, explicitly as an archived-but-available route.
