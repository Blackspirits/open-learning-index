# Maintenance Shallow Screening 11 — NAU post-payment-model access correction

## Trigger

On 6 July 2026 NAU announced a material platform change: partner organisations can now choose among fully free courses, open courses with paid assessment/certification, and advanced paid courses.

That current platform evidence is newer and more specific than the October 2025 generic certification policy used during maintenance screening 10.

## Correction

Five batch-10 advances remain **advance**, because their current public teaching scope is substantive enough for Deep Review, but their access classification is corrected from **F0** to conservative **F2**:

- `nau-fundamentos-bases-dados`
- `nau-estruturas-dados`
- `nau-algoritmos-complexidade`
- `nau-ciencia-aberta-dados-fair`
- `nau-lingua-portuguesa-escrita-academica`

Each current course page describes assessment/certification mechanics but does not explicitly establish, after the July payment change, that the complete assessed/certificate learner journey remains zero-cost.

The project therefore does **not** infer F0 from:
- historical NAU policy;
- the presence of a certificate section;
- public enrolment;
- PRR/public funding.

F2 is intentionally conservative until course-specific checkout/access evidence proves a stronger free tier.

## Courses not changed

- `nau-sustentabilidade-ambiental` remains **F0** because its current official course page explicitly states that the successful-completion certificate is **isento de emolumentos** and corresponds to 1 ECTS.
- `nau-introducao-programacao-conceitos-fundamentais` and `nau-iniciacao-lingua-portuguesa` remain **F2** because the official PortugalX/edX migration evidence explicitly distinguishes free Audit content from paid certificate/graded completion.

## State impact

No decision counts change:

- 213 advance
- 5 hold
- 88 reject
- 306 discovery candidates
- 131 canonical records

The eight pt-PT advances remain the next Deep Review batch. Their access scores must use these corrected current tiers.

## Gate

Proceed to Deep Review only from these superseding current screening records. Upgrade any F2 course later only if course-specific post-July-2026 evidence proves free assessment/certification.
