## Mathematics & Statistics

Eight candidates advance and one is held.

MIT 6.042J, the three independent-study calculus/differential-equations courses, MIT Complex Variables and MIT Theory of Numbers all advance. Their dates matter far less than package completeness because the mathematical foundations are stable; the self-study editions remain unusually strong in worked practice, assessment and solutions.

CMU Statistical Reasoning advances because its twelve interactive modules combine simulations, computer tutors, hints and immediate feedback, and an Open + Free independent learner route is still exposed. Khan Statistics and Probability also advances as a broad mastery-based route for a different learner level.

CMU Probability and Statistics is held. The current institutional page documents strong interactive pedagogy, but its access instructions are educator-oriented and the surfaced direct OLI registration evidence is legacy-dated. Independent free access must be re-established before advancement.

## Natural Sciences

Six candidates advance and one is rejected.

Both Yale physics courses advance because their core quantitative physics is stable and the complete sequences remain deep. Both Yale organic chemistry semesters also advance: archive age is a review factor, but structure, bonding, mechanism, spectroscopy and synthesis remain sufficiently foundational for direct comparison.

FAO's climate-smart agriculture course advances with a current technical pathway and series-level final-test/digital-badge mechanics. The EU Climate Action Academy advances especially strongly on currency: its page was published in September 2026 and is actively adding climate communication, citizen science, AI, policy and misinformation modules.

Yale *Frontiers and Controversies in Astrophysics* is rejected. It was recorded in 2007 specifically around rapidly advancing frontier questions, and even the provider's later update acknowledges the scale of subsequent research. This is a case where excellent teaching cannot compensate for obsolete scientific-state content.

## Engineering & Electronics

Three candidates advance, three are held and one is rejected.

MATLAB Onramp and Simulink Onramp advance as current, free, browser-based interactive foundations with automated feedback and completion certificates. Signal Processing Onramp also advances because the practical spectral-analysis/filtering skills are transferable enough to justify deeper comparison.

Stateflow Onramp and Image Processing Onramp are held rather than rejected: both are current and hands-on, but they are narrower and strongly vendor-specific. Their incremental value must be compared against broader control, embedded and computer-vision alternatives already in the pool.

Yale *Frontiers of Biomedical Engineering* is held for a scientific/regulatory currency audit. Its 25-lecture package is substantial, but a 2008 treatment of medical technologies, product development and FDA approval cannot be assumed current.

HP LIFE *3D Printing* is rejected from the engineering finalist pool. It remains a useful current free course with a certificate, but its focus is introductory business use of 3D printing for prototypes and products rather than technically deep additive-manufacturing education.

## Arts & Design

Five candidates advance, two are held and two are rejected.

Unity Essentials and Creative Core both advance: they are current, project-led pathways with shareable Pathway badges, and Creative Core reaches into lighting, materials, VFX, animation, audio and UI rather than mere tool onboarding. Khan AP/College Art History advances for broad global coverage. OpenLearn's 24-hour *A freelance career in the creative arts* advances for a distinctive career-practice role, and *Picturing the family* advances for stable visual-source criticism.

OpenLearn *Art and visual culture: medieval to modern* is held because its 2018 update and predominantly Western scope need direct comparison with the broader Khan route. *Recording music and sound* is held because practical production technology has moved since its 2018 update, even though several recording principles remain stable.

*Making sense of art history* is rejected as an older introductory route now dominated by broader art-history survivors. *The business of film* is also rejected: its material originated in 2015 and was last updated in 2020, while film distribution, platforms and industry economics have changed materially.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Mathematical age and scientific age are not equivalent.** A 2010 calculus course can remain first-rate while a 2007 “frontiers” science course can become unsuitable.
2. **Interactive feedback is a major differentiator.** CMU, Khan, MathWorks and Unity earn attention because learners do more than consume lectures.
3. **Vendor-specific does not mean low quality.** It becomes a portfolio and transferability penalty when several adjacent courses from the same ecosystem compete for limited finalist slots.
4. **Current access must be independently verifiable.** Strong pedagogy is not enough when the public learner route is ambiguous.
5. **Creative-industry courses need freshness checks too.** Film business and audio-production workflows can age materially even when artistic principles remain stable.
6. **A free credential cannot rescue insufficient depth.** HP LIFE 3D Printing is useful, but it does not become a top engineering course merely because completion is certified.
