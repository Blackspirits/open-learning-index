## Health & Medicine

Three candidates advance and one is held.

FAO *Improving nutrition through agriculture and food systems* advances as a specialist five-lesson systems course with scenario-based teaching, a final exam and a free digital badge. TGHN *Introduction to Good Clinical Laboratory Practice* also advances despite its short duration because it is a tightly bounded professional-standards course, assumes prior GCP knowledge and verifies mastery through an 80% final quiz.

WHO *Chemical Hazards Part II: Elements for Assessment* advances as part of a 2025 WHO emergency-readiness sequence. It teaches interpretation of environmental, clinical and classification evidence during early chemical-incident assessment and fills a specialist role not covered by the clinical-research survivors.

OpenLearn *Lottery of birth* is held. Its postgraduate-level interdisciplinary treatment of demography, health, inequality and policy is substantial, but it was last updated in 2020 and its evidence base is sufficiently time-sensitive to require a targeted currency review.

## Cybersecurity & IT

Two candidates advance, one is held and one is rejected.

OpenLearn *Introduction to cyber security: stay safe online* advances strongly after a March 2026 update: it offers 24 hours over eight weeks, weekly quizzes, a free digital badge and current treatment of threats, malware, network security, cryptography, identity theft and risk management.

IBM SkillsBuild *Cybersecurity Fundamentals* also advances. The current pathway is roughly seven hours, spans offensive and defensive fundamentals plus emerging AI impacts, and awards an IBM digital credential after a final assessment threshold.

Microsoft Learn *AI security fundamentals* is held rather than advanced automatically. Its topic is exceptionally current, but the entire path is only about 2 hours 10 minutes across three modules; its incremental value needs direct depth comparison against the stronger cybersecurity field.

AWS *Cloud Essentials Learning Plan* is rejected from the cybersecurity finalist pool. It is current and useful, but its primary subject is broad AWS cloud fundamentals — services, architecture, pricing, support and general security — so the candidate is a taxonomy mismatch rather than a top cybersecurity course.

## Psychology & Behaviour

One candidate is held and one is rejected.

OpenLearn *Living psychology: animal minds* is held. The 12-hour intermediate package gives meaningful comparative-psychology and research-methods depth, but its 2018 update date matters because animal cognition and Theory-of-Mind research remain empirical and active.

*Introduction to child psychology* is rejected. It is an eight-hour adapted extract, last updated in 2020, and is both narrower and less current than stronger introductory and specialist psychology survivors already in the pool.

## Marketing & Sales

One candidate advances and one is rejected.

HubSpot *Content Marketing Certification Course* advances despite provider concentration because it remains a genuinely substantial specialist route: 14 lessons, 54 videos, 11 quizzes and about 8 hours 23 minutes with free certification.

NAU / Accenture *Gestão de vendas* is rejected from the finalist pool. The pt-PT course is current, structured and certified, but at only two hours it does not provide enough depth to beat the stronger sales and marketing survivors. Original-language diversity cannot override the same quality bar.

## Education & Teaching

One candidate advances and one is rejected.

OpenLearn *Teaching and learning tricky topics* advances as a 24-hour, eight-week intermediate course with a free badge. Its threshold-concept framework is operationalised through needs analysis, structured mapping, assessment design and learning-design interventions, giving it substantial practice value.

OpenLearn *Open education* is rejected. The seven-week course has historical importance, but a 2012 publication centred on OER, MOOCs and digital openness is too exposed to changes in platforms, licensing practice, generative AI and online pedagogy to remain a best-current-course candidate without a major refresh.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Short does not always mean shallow.** A one-to-two-hour professional standards course can advance when its scope is tightly bounded, prerequisite-aware and formally assessed.
2. **Fast-moving novelty is not enough.** Microsoft AI Security is timely but still held because depth matters alongside currency.
3. **Taxonomy fit is part of quality control.** A strong cloud-foundation path should not survive merely because security is one of its modules.
4. **Empirical psychology needs stricter freshness than stable pedagogy.** An older teaching-method course can remain useful while an older cognition course needs scientific review.
5. **Language diversity never lowers the quality bar.** The pt-PT sales course is useful but too short to beat stronger alternatives.
