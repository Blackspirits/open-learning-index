- **Hugging Face Deep Reinforcement Learning Course** — provider-declared low-maintenance state, a non-functional unit and retired leaderboard make it too degraded for a best-current AI index.
- **freeCodeCamp A1 Professional Spanish** — still a public beta rather than a fully live complete certification.
- **freeCodeCamp A1 Professional Chinese** — same: public beta/incomplete status fails the language-completeness rule.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Resolve the blocker, not the prestige.** CMU advances because its independent route is now explicit; Berkeley is rejected because its practical route is institution-bound.
2. **External equipment is an accessibility cost, not automatically a paywall.** Nordic courses can proceed to deep review when hardware requirements are transparent, but they must lose accessibility points later.
3. **Time-bounded can still be current.** British Council and FUN advance while their active windows are open; monitoring must revisit them promptly.
4. **F0 claims are not necessary for advancement.** Fortinet can advance as F1 without asserting an unverified free certification exam.
5. **Beta is not complete.** Public availability does not make an unfinished language curriculum finalist-ready.
6. **A stale core legal fact can justify rejection.** Legal authority of the provider does not excuse outdated content.
