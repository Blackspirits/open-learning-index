## Methodological lessons

1. **Interactive does not automatically mean complete.** Practice quality and scope both matter.
2. **A tool vendor can still produce transferable education.** Canva Graphic Design Essentials and Figma advance because their learning outcomes extend beyond button-by-button product onboarding.
3. **Legacy usability is a legitimate rejection reason.** Historical prestige cannot compensate indefinitely for obsolete technical delivery.
4. **Language-learning completeness is strict.** Explicitly introductory or unfinished pathways do not compete fairly with A1–B1 or full structural courses.
5. **No certificate remains acceptable at F1/F2.** Strong practice and course completeness can outweigh the absence of a credential.
