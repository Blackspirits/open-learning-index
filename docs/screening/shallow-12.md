## Mandarin Chinese

### Advance — BLCU / UMOOCs: 初级汉语口语入门

The active 2026 run is explicitly free, runs for 18 weeks and contains 32 videos, 10 assessments and 32 downloadable resources. It is built for zero-beginner learners, beginning with pinyin before moving into functional everyday communication.

The course advances at **F1**. No free formal completion credential was confirmed, and the public page does not make the learner-support/explanation language explicit; both facts remain recorded for deep review. Its current active run and assessment density make it a substantially stronger gap-filler than the short Mandarin tasters previously researched.

### Advance — MandarinX / edX: Mandarin Chinese Level 1

The provider's current edX page explicitly states that MandarinX courses can be audited for free. Level 1 is a self-paced six-week beginner route with English mediation, tones, everyday spoken vocabulary and practical communication.

It advances at **F2** because verified certification and some enrichment are paid. The project does not conflate free audit access with a free credential or paid verified-track features.

The two Mandarin survivors now provide useful contrast for deep review:

- current time-bounded university course with assessments;
- self-paced English-mediated audit route.

## Arabic

### Advance — Madinah Arabic: Arabic Grammar Course

The course exposes more than 90 sequenced free grammar/morphology lessons, audio-supported examples and interactive marked exercises. A separate free Reading Course is explicitly provided as the prerequisite route for learners who do not yet know the script.

It advances at **F1**. Deep review must penalise its grammar-heavy skill balance where appropriate and examine the mixture of Modern Standard and Classical/Qur'anic examples, but neither issue undermines the unusually substantial free learning package.

### Advance — Al Jazeera Learning Arabic: General Language pathway

Al Jazeera explicitly describes the site as a free open educational source. The General Language area spans introductory through proficiency levels and contains a full letters-and-sounds sequence, daily-life material, grammar series, higher-level texts, interactive exercises/results and placement testing.

It advances at **F1** as an open learning experience rather than a conventional fixed course. The non-linear structure is a deep-review comparison factor, not a shallow-screen blocker. Paid courses offered separately by Al Jazeera Media Institute are not part of this record.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Corrective discovery should stop early.** Four strong candidates are better than filling an 8–12 allowance with weaker records.
2. **Free audit is not free certification.** MandarinX remains F2 even though the core course can be audited for free.
3. **Resources can qualify without pretending to be courses.** Al Jazeera is explicitly represented as a non-linear open-learning pathway.
4. **Specialist depth can close a coverage gap without pretending to cover all four skills.** Madinah Arabic advances for its exceptionally deep grammar/morphology route, with its skill imbalance recorded transparently.
5. **Time-bounded access is not automatically F3.** BLCU currently exposes the complete free course during an active long run; availability becomes a maintenance risk rather than a reason to misclassify the current learner path.
