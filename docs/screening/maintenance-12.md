## Access decision

NAU's 6 July 2026 policy defines:

1. fully free courses;
2. free/open content with paid assessment/certification;
3. paid advanced courses with only partial free access.

The current learner pages for these three courses do not identify their exact payment category.

NAU's June 2026 C-DAYS article describes its cybersecurity content as accessible, massive and free, which supports free teaching access for the two CNCS routes. It does not override the later course-specific payment policy for assessment/certification.

Therefore the index records **F2**, not F0/F1, until exact current checkout evidence establishes otherwise.
