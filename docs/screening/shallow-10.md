This tranche screens **29 candidates** across Languages, Finance & Economics, Business & Entrepreneurship, Law & Public Policy, and Writing & Communication. The gate is deliberately strict about microcourse depth, beta or unreleased curricula, archived time-bounded MOOCs, legal currency, jurisdiction dependence and whether a free credential adds enough learning value to beat stronger alternatives.

## Writing & Communication

Two candidates advance and two are rejected.

OpenLearn *Start writing fiction* advances after a May 2026 update and provides a practical 12-hour creative-writing route rather than another generic workplace-communication course. *Essay and report writing skills* also advances: its 2019 update is a review factor, but the underlying academic-writing skills are stable and the 15-hour structure remains substantial.

HP LIFE *Effective Presentations* and *Business Email* are rejected from the finalist pool. Both are legitimate current free courses with certificates, but the former is redundant with deeper presentation pathways already surviving and the latter is roughly a 30-minute workplace microcourse. Credential availability does not compensate for insufficient comparative depth.

## Languages

Three candidates advance, two are held and two are rejected.

OpenLearn *How to learn a language* advances for a distinctive 24-hour metacognitive role covering goals, motivation, vocabulary, four-skill development, resource evaluation and virtual immersion. freeCodeCamp A2 and B1 English for Developers also advance: both are live full pathways with final certification exams and a distinctive technical/professional context.

freeCodeCamp A1 Professional Spanish and A1 Professional Chinese are held. Both are exposed in production but remain labelled Beta, and their original rollouts were partial; completeness and certification must be verified end to end rather than inferred. A2 Professional Spanish is rejected because the current upstream curriculum configuration still places it in the **Upcoming** stage rather than the normal production curriculum.

OpenLearn *Getting started with French 1* is rejected despite being well made and credential-bearing because the provider explicitly describes it as an extract from the beginning of a fuller French course. It cannot compete fairly with complete French pathways already surviving.

## Business & Entrepreneurship

Two candidates advance, two are held and two are rejected.

OpenLearn *Entrepreneurship – from ideas to reality* advances strongly after a March 2026 update: it offers 24 hours across eight sessions, cases, quizzes, business models, funding, risk, capability building and pitching with free completion recognition. Universidade NOVA / NAU *Academia de Empreendedorismo* also advances: it is a current 30-hour pt-PT pathway with 18 video lessons, quizzes, business/finance/legal/IP coverage, a free diploma and **1 ECTS** at the stated completion threshold.

HP LIFE *Starting a Small Business* and *Inventory Management* are rejected for comparative redundancy: the first is a broad introductory starter already dominated by deeper entrepreneurship pathways; the second is a narrow topic already covered inside the stronger Saylor Operations Management route.

## Law & Public Policy

Four candidates advance and two are held.

WIPO *IP Panorama 2.0* advances as a current 2026, 20-hour open-access intellectual-property/business course. Its discovery access tier is corrected from F1 to **F2** because WIPO explicitly lists **no certificate** and a sufficiently strong free formal assessment path was not independently established.

Council of Europe HELP *Cybercrime and Electronic Evidence* and *Data Protection and Privacy Rights* both advance after the Council of Europe explicitly identified the Cybercrime and Data Protection courses as **updated in 2026**, materially reducing legal-currency risk. *Protection and Safety of Journalists* also advances with an April 2026 launch confirming roughly 10 hours, five modules, translations and an electronic Statement of Accomplishment.

OpenLearn *Legal skills and debates in Scotland* is held: its 24-hour badged structure is strong, but the material is Scotland-specific and was last updated in 2020. HELP *Freedom of Expression* is also held because the surfaced rebuild dates to 2020 and current Article 10/ECtHR case-law coverage requires direct verification before advancement.

## Methodological lessons

1. **A certificate is not a depth multiplier.** Current certified microcourses can still be rejected when stronger comprehensive alternatives exist.
2. **Beta and Upcoming are not equivalent to complete.** A public route or repository entry does not prove that a full learner pathway and credential are production-ready.
3. **Archived access changes eligibility.** A past free completion route does not satisfy the index when a new learner cannot currently enrol and complete it.
4. **Legal currency can change a decision materially.** A 2026 provider-confirmed update can resolve staleness risk; a 2020 legal course can remain on hold even when its pedagogy is strong.
5. **Discovery access claims remain provisional.** WIPO IP Panorama is corrected from F1 to F2 because current official evidence explicitly says no certificate.
6. **Original-language coverage must still clear the quality bar.** The pt-PT NAU course advances because it is substantial, assessed and current; language diversity alone would not be enough.
