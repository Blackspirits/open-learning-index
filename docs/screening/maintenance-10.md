# Maintenance Shallow Screening 10 — pt-PT challenger scan 01

## Scope

Shallow-screen all 12 records from Discovery batch 10 using current first-party evidence.

The purpose is not to increase Portuguese representation mechanically. A pt-PT course advances only when the current learner route, instructional substance and evidence quality justify expensive Deep Review under the same standard as every other language.

## Outcome

- **8 advance**
- **1 hold**
- **3 reject**

If merged, current shallow state becomes:

- **213 advance**
- **5 hold**
- **88 reject**
- **306 discovery candidates**

The canonical publication set remains unchanged at 131. Existing Deep Review / Phase-4 coverage becomes **205 / 213 current advances**, leaving these eight pt-PT advances as the next review debt.

## Advances

### Introdução à Programação: Conceitos Fundamentais — advance, F2

The NAU record is archived because PortugalX migrated to edX, not because the course disappeared.

Instituto Politécnico de Tomar currently points learners to a live edX edition. The 60-hour structure remains unusually substantial for a free pt-PT programming route: algorithmic problem solving, flowcharts, pseudocode and exercise design.

The access model changes materially after migration. Free edX audit gives the teaching content but does not provide the full graded completion route or a free certificate. The Discovery F0 impression is therefore corrected to **F2**.

Decision: **advance**.

### Fundamentos de Bases de Dados — advance, F0

Current NAU / Instituto Politécnico de Lisboa evidence shows the course open for enrolment.

The 25-hour route covers:

- conceptual, logical and physical relational modelling;
- normalization;
- SQL and transactions;
- PostgreSQL;
- Python/database integration;
- practical exercises.

The public 4 ECTS are restricted to eligible students in the wider PRODIGI programme and are **not** recorded as free public credit. NAU's current certification policy nevertheless confirms a free platform participation certificate for successful open-course completion.

Decision: **advance**.

### Estruturas de Dados — advance, F0

Current open 25-hour route covering lists, stacks, queues, heaps, hash tables, search trees, matrices and graphs, with exercises and a formal completion threshold.

The generic NAU certification policy resolves the certificate ambiguity left at Discovery: successful completion receives a free NAU participation certificate.

Decision: **advance**.

### Algoritmos e Complexidade — advance, F0

Current open 25-hour route covering complexity, sorting, divide-and-conquer, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms, tree traversal and graph algorithms.

The course is specialist enough to represent a real algorithms/theory role and includes practical exercises plus free NAU certification on successful completion.

Decision: **advance**.

### Ciência Aberta e Dados FAIR — advance, F0

Current open 20-hour course with six modules, module knowledge checks and a final assessment.

It covers open science, FAIR data, curation/stewardship, sensitive-data security, policy and responsible AI in research. Completion certification is free; the page claims no ECTS.

This is not a technical machine-learning course and does not close that documented gap.

Decision: **advance**.

### Sustentabilidade Ambiental — advance, F0

Current open 27-hour environmental course covering waste, water, energy, air quality and noise using videos, texts, tasks, quizzes and interactive activities.

The official page explicitly states that successful completion gives a certificate **isento de emolumentos** corresponding to **1 ECTS**. This is treated as verified provider-specific free credit, not inferred from generic NAU policy.

Decision: **advance**.

### Língua portuguesa em contexto de escrita académica — advance, F0

Current open ISCTE/NAU course, 30 hours, explicitly in the European-Portuguese variant.

It covers grammar, punctuation, formal oral and written communication, paragraphing, summary, synthesis and expository/explanatory writing, with formative assessment and completion certification.

This is productive writing instruction, not a token language-diversity slot.

Decision: **advance**.

### Iniciação à Língua Portuguesa — advance, F2

The former NAU route has moved to a live edX edition.

University of Porto / NAU evidence supports:

- explicit European Portuguese;
- no prior knowledge required;
- eight practical units;
- about 32 hours;
- everyday communication, pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar;
- continuing edition revision.

Free access on edX is to teaching content; verified certification and the full graded completion route are paid. The course therefore advances as **F2**, not F0.

Decision: **advance**.

## Hold

### Todos Contam e-Learning — hold, F2

This is the official Portuguese financial-education platform backed by the national financial supervisors and exposes substantial public content on budgeting, payments, saving/investment, credit, insurance, the financial system and digital finance.

The unresolved blocker is **currency**.

The public learner route does not expose reliable lesson-level revision dates. In finance, consumer protection and digital financial services, a live regulator-owned website is not enough evidence that every instructional unit is current in 2026.

Decision: **hold** until substantive revision/currentness evidence can be verified.

## Rejects

### Liderança, Gestão e Valorização de Pessoas — reject

The 39-hour syllabus is substantial, but current official NAU catalogue evidence marks the course **Arquivado**.

No live successor/open learner route was found.

Decision: **reject** on current availability.

### Oferta Pedagógica - Línguas Vivas Estrangeiras — reject

Universidade Aberta keeps the 16-hour experience permanently open, but those 16 hours are shared across German, Spanish, French and English.

The provider frames it as a brief illustration designed to stimulate interest. It does not constitute a serious pathway in any one target language and cannot challenge the deeper canonical language courses.

Decision: **reject** for insufficient depth/completeness.

### Cidadãos Estrangeiros: Trabalho e Formação — reject

The topic is useful and Portugal-specific, but current official NAU evidence marks the course **Arquivado**.

That blocks access before the additional legal/procedural currentness question is even reached.

Decision: **reject**.

## Access corrections from Discovery

Discovery is deliberately provisional. Shallow Screening makes two important corrections:

- `nau-introducao-programacao-conceitos-fundamentais`: apparent F0 → **F2**, because the current edX audit route does not include the free graded/certificate path.
- `nau-iniciacao-lingua-portuguesa`: apparent F0 → **F2** for the same migrated-edX access reason.
- `nau-estruturas-dados`: apparent F1 → **F0**, because the current NAU platform certification policy establishes free completion certification.

## Remaining pt-PT gaps after this gate

This pass materially improves the candidate pool, but it does **not** solve every Portuguese-language weakness:

- deep technical AI / machine learning remains open;
- broad, current legal education remains open;
- finance remains unresolved pending currentness evidence for Todos Contam;
- foreign-language learning **through pt-PT** remains weak: the only new multi-language university sampler is too shallow.

These gaps should stay visible rather than be filled with inferior courses.
