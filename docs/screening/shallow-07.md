## AI & Data

Eleven candidates advance and four are held.

Microsoft AI Concepts and Develop AI Agents, IBM AI/Data Fundamentals, both MathWorks Onramps, Cisco's two data pathways, both pt-PT/English NAU AI courses and the Hugging Face Agents Course advance. These candidates combine current subject coverage with assessment, hands-on work, credentials, localisation or distinctive practice.

Fast.ai Practical Deep Learning is held rather than rejected: its project-first pedagogy is excellent, but its flagship material is from 2022 and the framework/model landscape has moved rapidly.

Microsoft AI-901 is held because its one-day Azure-specific scope overlaps a stronger general Microsoft pathway; its marginal value should be established head-to-head.

Hugging Face Audio is held for a focused library/model-currency audit. Hugging Face Deep RL is held more strongly: the provider labels it low-maintenance, Unit 7 is non-functional and the leaderboard is retired, so practical completion must be verified before advancement.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Stable theory and software runtime age are different risks.** An older algorithms course can remain excellent while a beginner course tied to Python 3.5 becomes avoidably stale.
2. **Public visibility is not the same as public practical completeness.** Berkeley CS61C demonstrates why labs, repositories, autograders and institution-only services must be mapped explicitly.
3. **Fast-moving AI needs stronger currency gates.** A famous course can be held when its stack is several generations behind or its practical components are no longer maintained.
4. **Vendor-specific learning can still advance.** It should survive when the path is pedagogically complete and useful; transferability is scored later.
5. **Free credential claims need current course-specific evidence.** IBM's direct course-catalogue routes and NAU's current completion model remove earlier discovery ambiguity.
6. **pt-PT coverage is valuable but not exempt from quality gates.** The two NAU AI courses advance because they are current, assessed and coherent, not simply because they are Portuguese.
