# Methodology

## Two scores, two questions

### Quality Score

“How good is this learning resource on its own terms?”

Weighted components:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Pedagogy | 25% |
| Depth | 20% |
| Practice | 20% |
| Materials | 10% |
| Currency | 10% |
| Instructor/provider expertise | 10% |
| Accessibility | 5% |

`quality_score = Σ(component × weight)`

Currency is **subject-sensitive**. A 2011 linear algebra lecture does not become poor merely because it is old; an AI tooling course can become obsolete quickly.

### Recommendation Score

“How strongly should a learner choose this over realistic alternatives today?”

This is editorial and may account for prerequisites, time-to-value, fit, friction, duplicated coverage and availability. It must never silently replace the Quality Score.

## Tiers

- **S+**: 9.50–10.00 — reference-level, exceptional.
- **S**: 9.00–9.49 — excellent.
- **A+**: 8.50–8.99 — strongly recommended.
- **A**: 8.00–8.49 — very good.
- Below A: normally excluded from the published core index.

## Free-access taxonomy

- **F0_FULL_CREDENTIAL** — complete course/path and a free provider completion credential.
- **F1_FULL_ASSESSMENTS** — complete learning path with meaningful free labs/assessment, but no free formal credential.
- **F2_CONTENT_ONLY** — substantial complete teaching content, but no free formal completion path.
- **F3_PARTIAL_PREVIEW** — trial, preview, first module only or otherwise materially incomplete. Excluded from main ranking.

“Certificate” and “academic credit” are stored separately. A badge is not ECTS, and neither automatically increases pedagogical quality.

## Evidence standard

Prefer official course/provider documentation. Use independent reviews or community discussion only as supplementary evidence for learner experience, not as the sole proof of pricing, credits or access.
