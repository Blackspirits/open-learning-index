## Route hygiene before scoring

The original batch plan included:

- `ibm-skillsbuild-ai-fundamentals`
- `ibm-skillsbuild-data-fundamentals`

Both former course-specific URLs now redirect to the generic IBM SkillsBuild university catalog. Current IBM/Credly evidence still indicates that the credentials exist, so the courses were not rejected. However, an exact stable learner-facing enrolment route could not be independently verified.

They were therefore moved from `advance` to `hold` before Deep Review. This is the intended protocol behaviour: route uncertainty is resolved at the shallow gate rather than hidden inside a score.

## Head-to-head signals

### Programming foundations

`nand2tetris` is a category-leader candidate. Its twelve projects force learners to construct the hardware and software stack layer by layer, producing unusually strong conceptual integration and practical depth.

`berkeley-cs61a-fall-2026` remains stronger as a rigorous programming-language and abstraction course; Nand2Tetris is stronger for understanding how computer systems fit together. They are complements, not duplicates.

`saylor-cs101-programming-i` is useful and accessible, especially for Java learners who value a free credential, but does not match the project intensity or pedagogical integration of the leaders.

### freeCodeCamp v9

The current freeCodeCamp repository confirms that the v9 certifications use interactive lessons, workshops, labs, reviews and quizzes, followed by **five required projects** and a certification exam.

All three reviewed v9 paths therefore score strongly on practice:

- Python: strongest general beginner programming option among the three;
- Relational Databases: strong specialist database route;
- Responsive Web Design: exceptionally accessible HTML/CSS path.

They do not automatically beat Odin. Odin remains stronger for local developer workflow, Git, independent research and larger end-to-end development habits; freeCodeCamp wins on hosted accessibility and free formal certification.

### AI & Data

`cisco-introduction-data-science` is a legitimate six-hour assessed introduction, but the already reviewed `cisco-data-analytics-essentials` is the substantially stronger recommendation for serious learners.

`mathworks-deep-learning-onramp` is pedagogically strong because learners train and evaluate models directly in the browser, but it is narrow and vendor-specific.

`nau-introducao-inteligencia-artificial` is valuable for pt-PT access and beginner literacy, not for technical competence. Its three-hour duration correctly produces the lowest Quality Score in this batch.

### Cybersecurity

`fortinet-fortigate-operator` is strong operational training, current to FortiOS 7.6 and practice-oriented. It should not displace general foundations such as OpenLearn because its value depends on a Fortinet-specific job or environment.

### Specialist technical courses

`fun-web-semantique-donnees` has excellent academic expertise and meaningful graded practice around RDF, SPARQL, OWL and related standards. Its recommendation is limited mainly by specialist scope and French-only delivery.

`nordic-matter-fundamentals` demonstrates the Quality/Recommendation split particularly clearly: near-9 intrinsic quality, but an 8.0 recommendation because the full practical route requires specific Nordic development boards and, for Windows/macOS users, a Raspberry Pi.
