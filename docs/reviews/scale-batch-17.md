## Main conclusions

### General marketing

`Saylor BUS203` is the strongest broad foundation. It covers segmentation/targeting/positioning, research, product, pricing, distribution, promotion, campaign planning and social media across about 30 hours. End-of-unit assessments are repeatable, the final exam/certificate are free, and the syllabus was last modified on **2 October 2025**.

### AEO / AI search

`hubspot-aeo-fundamentals` is the most current route in the category. It directly covers answer engines, E-E-A-T, AI citations, structured data, Search Console/GA4 and measurement limitations. Its **9.0 Recommendation** is intentionally higher than its **8.39 Quality** because it is unusually timely but only 2h20 and less practice-heavy than broader routes.

### Email marketing

`hubspot-email-marketing` is the strongest channel-specific email route, combining nine quizzes, one practical exercise, automation, testing, analytics and privacy. Email fundamentals age more slowly than social-platform interfaces.

### Revenue operations

`hubspot-revenue-operations` is the strongest specialist RevOps route: nearly seven hours covering process alignment, SLAs, accountability, team structure and strategy iteration across marketing, sales and customer-success operations.

### Social media and digital advertising

Both remain useful but receive explicit currentness penalties. Social Media Marketing still exposes legacy platform references such as Twitter, while digital-advertising interfaces, targeting options, privacy rules and measurement practices change rapidly.

### Inbound sales

Inbound Sales is a strong concise consultative-selling foundation, but its 3h06 scope does not make it a comprehensive professional sales-development programme.

## Provider concentration

Seven of eight current survivors are HubSpot courses. Phase 3 does **not** solve that by distorting scores. Provider concentration is a Phase 4 admission risk: final publication should admit multiple HubSpot routes only where they add clearly distinct value.

## Currency rules reinforced

1. Live availability is weaker evidence than a documented content refresh.
2. Fast-moving novelty can raise Recommendation without raising Depth.
3. Channel stability differs: email ages more slowly than social/ad-platform mechanics.
4. Broad foundations and specialist channel courses should not be compared as if they serve identical learner goals.
5. Free certification is an access benefit, not a Quality multiplier.
