## Main conclusions

### Best broad foundation

`Saylor BUS203` is the best general marketing foundation in the survivor pool because it covers the full process rather than one channel:

- segmentation, targeting and positioning;
- customer and market research;
- product life cycles;
- pricing;
- distribution and channels;
- promotion;
- campaign planning;
- social media.

It is estimated at 30 hours, includes repeatable end-of-unit assessments and a free final exam/certificate, and its syllabus was last modified on **2 October 2025**.

HubSpot is stronger for specialist channel execution; Saylor is stronger for a coherent all-round foundation.

### Best HubSpot specialist route

`hubspot-content-marketing` has the strongest intrinsic specialist package:

- 14 lessons;
- 54 videos;
- 11 quizzes;
- 8h23 of material;
- content strategy, ideation, storytelling, distribution, repurposing and measurement.

Its main caveat is currentness transparency. The route is live, but HubSpot does not expose a clear revision date. Core content strategy is durable; AI/search distribution is changing much faster.

### AEO Fundamentals

`hubspot-aeo-fundamentals` is the most current marketing candidate in the category.

The 2026 route explicitly covers:

- answer engines and AI search;
- E-E-A-T;
- AI citations;
- content structure for AI visibility;
- structured data;
- Search Console and GA4;
- limitations of current AEO measurement.

Its **9.0 Recommendation** is higher than its **8.39 Quality** because it is unusually timely and useful, while still only 2h20 and less practice-heavy than the broader survivors.

This is an intentional separation between current recommendation value and intrinsic course depth.

### Email Marketing

`hubspot-email-marketing` is the strongest channel-specific course for email.

It includes:

- 12 lessons;
- 31 videos;
- 9 quizzes;
- one practical exercise;
- personalization;
- automation;
- copy/design;
- analytics;
- A/B testing;
- privacy.

Email fundamentals are more stable than social-platform interfaces, which supports a stronger Currency score.

### Revenue Operations

`hubspot-revenue-operations` is the strongest specialist RevOps route in the pool.

Its almost seven-hour package covers process alignment, SLAs, accountability, team structure, friction and strategy iteration across marketing, sales and customer-success operations.

It is especially useful for learners whose role crosses functional boundaries.

### Social Media Marketing

The social-media course remains substantial and unusually well assessed:

- 10 lessons;
- 38 videos;
- 29 quizzes.

However, fast-moving platform content receives an explicit currency penalty. The live course still surfaces references such as Twitter alongside newer channels. This does not invalidate the strategic teaching, but current platform-specific supplementation is necessary.

### Digital Advertising

The Digital Advertising certification is a sound strategic introduction to:

- paid-media planning;
- journey-based advertising;
- targeting;
- creative;
- conversion paths;
- social/programmatic advertising;
- performance measurement.

Its limitation is structural: ad-platform interfaces, targeting options, privacy restrictions and measurement practices can change quickly. Recommendation therefore stays below the strongest category finalists.

### Inbound Sales

Inbound Sales is useful and well produced, but deliberately compact.

The 3h06 route is best understood as a consultative/inbound selling foundation, not as a complete sales-development programme.

## Provider concentration

Seven of eight current survivors are HubSpot courses.

This concentration is **not** resolved by artificially lowering strong courses in Phase 3. Instead:

- individual Quality scores reflect each learning experience;
- direct overlap and provider concentration remain explicit Phase 4 admission risks;
- Saylor BUS203 provides an important provider-independent broad foundation;
- final publication should avoid admitting multiple HubSpot routes unless each adds genuinely distinct value.

## Currency rules reinforced

1. **Live availability is not enough.**
   - A live social-media or digital-advertising course can still contain dated platform references.

2. **Fast-moving novelty can raise Recommendation without raising Depth.**
   - AEO Fundamentals is highly recommendable in 2026 despite compact scope.

3. **Channel stability differs.**
   - Email strategy ages more slowly than social/ad-platform mechanics.

4. **General foundations and specialist courses serve different roles.**
   - Saylor BUS203 is the best broad foundation.
   - HubSpot Content, Email, RevOps and AEO are specialist routes.

5. **Free certification is not a Quality multiplier.**
   - Scores remain driven by pedagogy, depth, practice, materials, currency, expertise and accessibility.
