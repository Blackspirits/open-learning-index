- core microeconomic theory, accounting mechanics and valuation concepts can remain useful for years;

- recent enrolment or platform availability is not treated as evidence that course content itself is current.

## General microeconomics head-to-head

### MRU Principles of Microeconomics

`mru-principles-microeconomics` is the strongest default introductory microeconomics recommendation.

The current route exposes:

- 94 lessons;
- 88 quizzes;
- 16 interactives;
- a free certificate;
- coverage from supply/demand and elasticity through trade, externalities, market structure, labor markets, public goods, asymmetric information and consumer choice.

Its main uncertainty is maintenance transparency: the course is active in 2026, but no explicit recent revision date is exposed.

That does not materially undermine the stable core theory, but it prevents a near-perfect Currency score.

### Saylor ECON101

`saylor-econ101-principles-microeconomics` is the strongest conventional alternative.

The current syllabus:

- estimates 32 hours;
- contains eight units;
- was last modified on 3 October 2025;
- includes repeatable end-of-unit assessments;
- includes a free final exam and free certificate;
- separates the optional paid ACE-credit exam from the free learning route.

Saylor therefore has a clearer recent-maintenance signal than MRU, but MRU wins pedagogically on scale and interaction.

### MIT 14.03

`mit-14-03-microeconomic-theory-public-policy-2025` is not a beginner alternative.

It requires introductory microeconomics and moves into:

- applied theory;
- causal inference and research design;
- data analysis;
- minimum wages;
- healthcare;
- trade;
- externalities;
- insurance and information problems;
- discrimination;
- machine learning and algorithmic decision-making.

The Fall 2025 materials include lecture slides, readings, class polls, problem sets, data and exams/solutions.

Some public problem sets/exams are retained from the older 2014 edition, which is explicitly recorded rather than hidden. The 2025 teaching frame and readings remain sufficiently current for a very high Currency score.

For prepared learners, this is the strongest economics course in the batch.

## General macroeconomics head-to-head

### MRU Principles of Macroeconomics

`mru-principles-macroeconomics` provides:

- 84 lessons;
- 78 quizzes;
- 8 interactives;
- free completion certification.

The route covers GDP, growth, savings/investment, the financial system, personal finance, unemployment, inflation, money, monetary policy, the Federal Reserve, the federal budget and fiscal policy.

It is the strongest general macroeconomics recommendation because of its practice density.

US-specific policy examples and the lack of an explicit revision date prevent a higher Currency score.

### Saylor ECON102

`saylor-econ102-principles-macroeconomics` is a strong alternative:

- 27 hours;
- current syllabus last modified 3 October 2025;
- repeatable unit assessments;
- free final exam and certificate;
- optional paid ACE credit clearly separated.

Saylor's currentness is easier to audit, while MRU remains stronger pedagogically.

### MIT Economic Crises

`mit-14-454-economic-crises-2026` is a specialist graduate route, not a principles-course replacement.

The Spring 2026 course covers:

- financial amplification;
- intermediation and credit crunches;
- coordination failures and bank runs;
- speculative bubbles and credit booms;
- liquidity traps and deleveraging;
- sudden stops and contagion.

The original course requirements put 70% of the grade on a long problem set with weekly deadlines. OCW publishes the problem set and solutions.

Its only serious recommendation constraint is accessibility: MIT 14.453 or equivalent graduate macroeconomics is a genuine prerequisite.

## Personal finance

### Khan Financial Literacy

`khan-financial-literacy` is the strongest current personal-finance route in the pool.

Current evidence shows:

- 16 units;
- 123 skills;
- quizzes and unit tests;
- a cumulative Course Challenge;
- mastery tracking;
- budgeting, credit, debt, insurance, investing, retirement, scams, education, taxes, employment, banking, cars and housing.

Khan announced a major update in January 2026 with new lessons across every unit and current real-world topics including digital payments, tax filing and unemployment.

The principal caveat is jurisdiction: many credit, tax, retirement, insurance and banking details are US-specific.

The lack of a formal certificate is not treated as a pedagogical weakness.

Previous personal-finance challengers are already outside the advance pool:

- MRU Money Skills was too narrow;
- OpenLearn MSE Academy of Money was rejected after its 2022 UK-specific tax/pension/mortgage content failed the currentness gate.

## Accounting head-to-head

### Saylor BUS103

`saylor-bus103-financial-accounting` is the strongest broad accounting route currently surviving.

The current syllabus:

- estimates 63 hours;
- covers ten units;
- was last modified on 26 September 2025;
- teaches transaction recording, adjustments, the accounting cycle, financial statements, inventory, receivables/payables, PPE, liabilities, equity and cash flows;
- includes repeatable unit assessments;
- provides a free final exam and certificate.

The optional ACE-credit proctoring route costs money and is kept explicitly separate from the free course.

### OpenLearn Fundamentals of accounting

`openlearn-fundamentals-accounting` is a better low-friction introduction:

- eight hours;
- four-week scaffold;
- bookkeeping/accounting distinction;
- basic numeracy;
- accounting equation;
- double-entry;
- ledgers;
- trial balance;
- balance sheet and income statement;
- downloadable PDF/EPUB/Word formats;
- free Statement of Participation.

However, the course was last formally updated in August 2019.

The double-entry core remains valid, so age is not a shallow rejection criterion, but Saylor is the stronger 2026 default because it is deeper and recently maintained.

The older MIT 15.501 accounting archive was already rejected at shallow screening due avoidable standards-currency risk.

## Finance theory

### MIT 15.401 Finance Theory I

`mit-15-401-finance-theory-i` is a useful example of separating intrinsic quality from current recommendation.

The Fall 2008 package remains exceptional:

- graduate-level lectures;
- recitations;
- lecture slides;
- problem-solving notes;
- fixed income;
- equities;
- futures and forwards;
- options;
- portfolio theory;
- CAPM/APT;
- capital budgeting;
- efficient markets;
- problem sets and solutions;
- midterm/final materials and solutions.

MIT added additional problem sets in 2019, improving the independent-study package.

Its **9.04 Quality** reflects depth, teaching, practice and materials.

Its **8.6 Recommendation** reflects the fact that a 2008 finance course predates major post-crisis regulation, market-structure changes, ETF growth and more recent empirical finance developments.

The course remains excellent for durable financial theory, but should not be treated as a current all-in-one finance curriculum.

## Currency rules reinforced

This batch reinforces several Phase 3 rules:

1. **Old does not automatically mean obsolete.**
   - MIT Finance Theory I remains exceptionally strong on stable theory.
   - OpenLearn Accounting remains sound on double-entry fundamentals.

2. **Age matters more when the course makes current-world claims.**
   - MIT Finance receives a material Currency penalty.
   - Khan Financial Literacy benefits strongly from its 2026 refresh.

3. **Current platform availability is weaker evidence than an explicit revision date.**
   - MRU courses remain highly recommendable but their Currency scores are conservative because update dates are not transparent.
   - Saylor and Khan receive stronger currentness credit from explicit 2025/2026 maintenance evidence.

4. **Jurisdiction is not the same as poor quality.**
   - Khan's US tax/retirement/credit content and MRU's US policy examples remain useful, but recommendation notes explicitly limit transferability.

5. **Credentials do not inflate Quality.**
   - MIT's F2 courses can outscore credentialed routes on teaching, depth and practice.
   - Free certificates are recorded as access/completion benefits, not quality multipliers.
