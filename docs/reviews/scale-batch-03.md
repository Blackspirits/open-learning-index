## Evidence gate: WHO Chemical Hazards Part II

`whoacademy-chemical-hazards-part-2` remains a current shallow `advance`.

WHO confirms that:

- Chemical Hazards Part II: Elements for Assessment is an official WHO Academy course;
- it is part of the current chemical-hazards series;
- the series is free of charge;
- Part II teaches learners to interpret environmental, clinical and classification evidence for chemical-release assessment.

The official learner URL also resolves to WHO Academy.

However, the Academy page is JavaScript-only and the publicly retrievable evidence does not currently expose enough granular syllabus, practice and assessment detail to support seven evidence-backed component scores without inference.

**Decision:** defer Deep Review rather than fabricate evidence. No shallow status change is justified because availability and free access are verified.

## Head-to-head signals

### Nordic embedded networking

The Nordic comparison family is now substantially complete.

- `nordic-cellular-iot-fundamentals` has the strongest scope in this batch: eight lessons, secure MQTT/CoAP, GNSS, modem tracing and a tracker project.
- `nordic-wifi-fundamentals` is similarly strong for Wi-Fi 6, provisioning, sockets, secure application protocols and power management.
- `nordic-matter-fundamentals` from batch 02 remains a close peer.

All three show the same calibrated behaviour: high Quality, materially lower Recommendation because full practice requires paid/specific hardware.

### Statistics

`cmu-oli-probability-statistics` demonstrates subject-sensitive currency correctly.

The course was last updated in 2017, but introductory probability, study design and inference are stable foundations. Its full-semester scope, simulations, cases, interactive exercises and StatTutor labs justify a high Quality Score. The missing certificate and scored tests in the Open & Free route do not reduce pedagogy.

Direct comparison should continue against:

- `cmu-oli-statistical-reasoning`
- `khan-statistics-probability`

### Teacher development

The two British Council courses are both current 2026 routes with free certificates, workbooks and three self-study modules.

`british-council-communicative-tasks` edges ahead because its public outcomes require more observable classroom design work: lesson stages, communicative activities, assessment tasks and task-based-learning design.

`british-council-understanding-language-systems` remains a strong specialist route for functional language and grammar pedagogy.

Both are time-bounded and must be rechecked after September 2026.

### FAO nutrition / food systems

The FAO comparison demonstrates why credentials cannot inflate Quality.

`fao-nutrition-food-security-livelihoods` is still active and currently issues digital badges, but FAO describes the module as only about 30–35 minutes and dates it to 2015. It is useful as a prerequisite, not a finalist-level standalone learning experience.

`fao-improving-nutrition-agri-food` is the stronger resource because its five lessons and scenario-based design apply concepts to nutrition-sensitive programmes, investments and policies.

### Clinical laboratory practice

`tghn-introduction-gclp` is a legitimate specialist orientation with professional peer review, multilingual access, an 80% certificate threshold and strong domain expertise.

Its one-to-two-hour duration correctly limits Depth and Practice. Recommendation remains relatively strong because the course solves a narrow professional need efficiently.

### Leadership and culture

`saylor-prdv231-ethical-leadership` survives as a differentiated ethics-focused course rather than another generic leadership path, but its public evidence shows limited experiential practice.

`fun-la-musique-quelle-histoire` is a strong multimedia survey with listening, scores, contextual history and quizzes. Its content is comparatively stable; the main constraints are French-only delivery and a current course closure date of 1 November 2026.
