### Blender Fundamentals 4.5 LTS

The current Blender Studio page explicitly states that the course is **still under construction**, with some chapters missing and existing chapters still being iterated. This contradicts the current shallow record's `completeness=complete` assumption.

Do not Deep Review until shallow status is corrected.

### OpenLearn — An introduction to music theory

The stable theory content remains educationally useful, but the live OpenLearn material still directs learners to trainers dependent on **Adobe Flash Player and/or Java Runtime Environment**. That is a material current-usability problem and contradicts the assumption that the complete practice route is cleanly usable today.

Do not Deep Review until shallow status is corrected or the obsolete-trainer dependency is shown to be nonessential to the current learner path.

## Head-to-head signals

### Creative technology

`unity-creative-core` is the strongest current real-time 3D creative pathway in the pool. It is ten weeks long, project-heavy, and currently exposes Unity 6.3 content across materials, lighting, animation, VFX, cameras, post-processing, audio, UI and prototyping.

`unity-essentials` is the better zero-experience onboarding route, while Creative Core is the stronger portfolio/practice experience.

### Animation and storytelling

`khan-pixar-in-a-box` remains exceptional despite older production examples. The current course exposes 15 units and 69 skills, with authentic Pixar production problems spanning storytelling, lighting, simulation, rigging, animation, modelling and rendering. Its educator guide also documents substantial hands-on activities and a roughly 12-hour complete storytelling lesson.

It complements rather than duplicates Unity: Pixar teaches transferable art/technical reasoning; Unity teaches a current production toolchain.

### Digital design

`figma-getting-started-design` clearly outperforms the Canva microcourse as a general design-learning recommendation. Figma explicitly frames the course as design education rather than product onboarding and covers audience research, ethics, accessibility, visual systems, UI, prototyping and exercises.

`canva-graphic-design-essentials` is current (updated 5 October 2025), free and certified, but its 53-minute duration caps Depth and Practice regardless of vendor prestige or credential.

### Music

`musictheory-net-lessons` is the strongest pure theory/practice system because its free exercise bank spans notation, construction, keyboard/fretboard identification and ear training.

Ableton's two browser courses are stronger for experiential creation:

- Learning Music: rhythm, melody, harmony, basslines and song structure;
- Learning Synths: synthesis and sound design through direct manipulation.

These three routes are complementary rather than redundant.

### Art history and visual literacy

`khan-ap-college-art-history` is the broad category leader for art history because it reaches across global traditions and contemporary material with strong visual-analysis resources.

`openlearn-picturing-family` is narrower but distinctive as training in photographic source criticism, bias and historical interpretation.

### Creative careers

`openlearn-freelance-creative-arts` provides a valuable 24-hour career-practice complement: audience research, promotion, investment, business structures and business planning. Its 2021 update date requires caution around tax/legal/platform-specific details, but the broader freelance-planning pedagogy remains useful.

## Methodological lessons

1. **Current vendor courses can be genuinely transferable.** Figma and Unity survive because learners practice broader design/creative skills, not merely buttons.
2. **A certificate cannot compensate for tiny scope.** Canva remains useful but scores below longer pathways.
3. **Stable artistic principles and fast-changing creative tooling need different currency treatment.**
4. **Incomplete current content is a shallow-screening blocker, not a score penalty.** Blender stays outside Deep Review until fixed.
5. **Obsolete technical dependencies are usability blockers even when the underlying theory is stable.** OpenLearn Music Theory requires maintenance before scoring.
