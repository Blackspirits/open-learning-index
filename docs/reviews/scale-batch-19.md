## Optimization Onramp

MathWorks provides a useful different model from the university courses already reviewed:

- free for everyone;
- browser-based MATLAB environment;
- no paid MATLAB license required for the course;
- hands-on exercises with automated feedback;
- practical optimization problems;
- shareable completion certificate.

The main limitation is not quality but scope and transferability. Roughly two hours cannot provide the theory depth of a full optimization course, and the workflow is explicitly MATLAB-specific.

That produces a respectable **8.65 Quality**, but a more restrained **8.4 Recommendation** for general learners.

## MIT 18.04 Complex Variables

## MIT 18.781 Theory of Numbers

The Spring 2012 course covers:

- divisibility and Euclidean algorithm;
- prime numbers;
- congruences;
- Chinese remainder theorem;
- factoring and RSA-related applications;
- primitive roots;
- quadratic residues and reciprocity;
- nine problem sets with solutions;
- midterm/final practice with solutions.

Again, archive age is not a meaningful defect for the mathematical core. Its recommendation is limited by specialist scope and proof prerequisites, not stale content.

It scores **9.22 Quality / 9.1 Recommendation**.
