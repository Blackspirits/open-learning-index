## Entrepreneurship head-to-head

### OpenLearn — Entrepreneurship: from ideas to reality

`openlearn-entrepreneurship-ideas-reality` is the strongest general entrepreneurship recommendation in the current survivor pool.

The route provides:

- 24 hours across eight sessions;
- business-model development;
- customer and market work;
- value proposition;
- resources and capabilities;
- finance and business structures;
- growth and networks;
- pitching;
- case studies;
- interactive quizzes;
- Business Model Canvas and profit-and-loss resources;
- free Statement of Participation and digital badge.

Most importantly for a medium-change field, the course was formally updated on **3 March 2026**.

This combination of currentness, practice and accessibility produces **9.08 Quality / 9.3 Recommendation**.

### MIT 15.393 — Nuts and Bolts of New Ventures

The January 2025 MIT IAP course is the strongest compact current startup-execution route.

It covers:

- customer identification;
- venture/business models;
- sales and presenting;
- people and organisational issues;
- startup legal issues;
- negotiation;
- financing;
- financial projections;
- common startup mistakes.

The sessions are taught by people with direct venture experience.

OCW also added **The Blues-Print for Innovation in 2026**, providing a direct maintenance signal.

The public route is, however, more lecture/practitioner driven than assessment driven, so Practice is deliberately below MIT 15.390 and OpenLearn.

### MIT 15.390 — New Enterprises

This is the strongest intrinsic project-based entrepreneurship package in the group.

The Spring 2013 course includes:

- semester-length venture development;
- customer and market analysis;
- product and competitive positioning;
- sales and go-to-market;
- organisation and legal formation;
- **12 assignments**;
- evaluation rubrics;
- financial templates;
- a detailed business plan;
- investor-pitch work.

That produces very high Pedagogy and Practice.

However, entrepreneurship is not a fully stable discipline. Startup financing, digital distribution, legal context, platform economics and common venture practices have changed materially since 2013.

The course therefore scores **9.02 Quality** but only **8.6 Recommendation**.

This is intentional separation between durable teaching quality and a current all-in-one recommendation.

### HP LIFE — Effective Leadership

This course illustrates why credentials and currentness cannot substitute for depth.

It is:

- free;
- active;
- certificate-bearing;
- highly accessible;
- current, with HP announcing the present course in January 2024 and continuing to promote it in 2026.

But HP LIFE's catalogue describes its stand-alone courses as roughly **30–60 minutes**.

The module includes useful material on:

- leadership versus management;
- communication;
- teams;
- change;
- ethics;
- personal leadership philosophy;
- a short self-awareness quiz.

That is appropriate for an introductory microcourse, not a serious leadership-development programme.

It therefore scores **7.45 Quality / 7.8 Recommendation** rather than being inflated by the certificate.

## Currency rules reinforced

This batch adds several useful Phase 3 principles:

1. **Entrepreneurship ages faster than its core concepts suggest.**
   - Customer discovery remains useful in MIT 15.390.
   - Startup finance, legal, distribution and ecosystem context from 2013 still require supplementation.

2. **Recent content can outperform prestigious archive content on Recommendation without outperforming it on every intrinsic dimension.**
   - MIT 15.390 has extraordinary project design.
   - OpenLearn 2026 is the better general current recommendation.

3. **Microcourses must remain microcourses in scoring.**
   - HP LIFE Effective Leadership is useful but cannot compete on Depth or Practice with 24-hour leadership programmes.

4. **Certificates do not inflate pedagogy.**
   - HP, Saylor and OpenLearn credentials are recorded as access/completion benefits.
   - MIT F2 courses remain competitive because of their actual teaching design.

5. **Language diversity has real index value but does not override depth.**
   - OpenClassrooms provides a useful current French project-management route.
   - NAU provides a substantive pt-PT entrepreneurship route.
