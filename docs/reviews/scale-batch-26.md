# Deep Review scale batch 26 — pt-PT legal and cybersecurity challengers

## Scope

Deep Review the three current advances from pt-PT maintenance Discovery batch 11.

The batch is deliberately role-sensitive:
- `Regulação Digital` is a specialist technology-regulation course;
- `Responsável de Cibersegurança` is an organisational cyber-governance/compliance route;
- `Cibersegurança para Executivos: Preparação para a NIS2` is an executive leadership and resilience route.

They are not scored as substitutes for hacking, network-defense or broad general-law courses.

## Regulação Digital — 8.48 / 8.8

The eight-hour duration is a real depth constraint, but the course covers a broader current technology-regulation surface than a simple awareness module:

- European digital-regulation context;
- platforms and digital markets;
- data regulation;
- AI and emerging technology;
- content moderation and e-commerce;
- accessibility;
- compliance tools and implementation.

Ten modules, mandatory learning checks and a final assessment give it enough structure to be a serious professional specialist.

Council of Europe HELP privacy and AI/human-rights routes remain stronger in their narrower legal specialisms and have verified F0 completion. Regulação Digital adds a different pt-PT cross-regulatory role.

## Responsável de Cibersegurança — 8.78 / 9.0

This is the clearest replacement for the stale Portuguese cybersecurity/legal material the project previously rejected.

Fifteen hours cover:

- national/EU cybersecurity law;
- risk analysis and management;
- technical and organisational controls;
- audit;
- asset inventories;
- incident management;
- policy and planning;
- annual reporting;
- compliance.

The original assessment design is meaningful: continuous module work 40% plus final exam 60%.

Its strongest dimension is currentness. Portugal's new Regime Jurídico da Cibersegurança was approved in December 2025 and entered into force in 2026, with implementation guidance still being operationalised by CNCS.

Cisco Cyber Threat Management remains stronger for labs and free credential mechanics. This course is stronger for Portuguese legal/governance context.

## Cibersegurança para Executivos: Preparação para a NIS2 — 8.81 / 9.1

This is the highest-recommendation course in the batch.

At 30 hours it is unusually substantial for executive cybersecurity and addresses:

- NIS/NIS2;
- incident response and continuity;
- supply-chain security;
- secure acquisition/development/maintenance;
- vulnerability management;
- cryptography;
- access controls/MFA;
- cyber-hygiene.

It is explicitly for executives and leaders seeking a holistic non-technical perspective.

The overlap with Responsável de Cibersegurança is acceptable because the learning role is different: the first prepares an operational organisational security-responsibility function; this one prepares leadership/governance decision makers.

## Access

All three remain conservative F2.

NAU guarantees free Audit access as a platform option, but since 6 July 2026 each partner can choose its assessment/certification/payment model. These course pages expose assessment and certificate thresholds without explicitly proving that the assessed completion route is zero-cost.

Quality and Recommendation therefore do not receive an F0 accessibility assumption.
