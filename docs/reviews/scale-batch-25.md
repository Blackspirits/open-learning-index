# Deep Review scale batch 25 — pt-PT challengers

## Scope

Deep Review the eight current advances from the first dedicated European-Portuguese maintenance challenger wave.

The review applies the standard 25/20/20/10/10/10/5 Quality weights and explicitly avoids giving intrinsic Quality points merely for language representation. European-Portuguese access can raise Recommendation only where it serves a real learner need that the stronger English-language incumbent cannot substitute.

## Computer Science

### Introdução à Programação: Conceitos Fundamentais — 8.52 / 8.7

The 60-hour scope is a serious learning route, not a short introductory MOOC. Its strongest feature is the use of algorithms, flowcharts/pseudocode and the Algorithmi environment to force computational reasoning before language syntax.

It does not beat the canonical beginner-programming leaders:

- CS50x is much broader and more project-heavy;
- CS50P provides stronger language-specific production;
- Helsinki Python has far denser exercise coverage and a full programming progression.

The pt-PT role is nevertheless genuine. The current edX Audit model is the largest penalty: teaching content is free, but graded assessment/certification are not.

### Fundamentos de Bases de Dados — 8.59 / 8.6

This is stronger than a narrow SQL tutorial because it includes:

- entity-association modelling;
- relational transformation;
- normalization;
- relational algebra;
- SQL and elementary transactions;
- PostgreSQL + Python integration.

CS50 SQL remains the stronger global database recommendation because it has denser independent practice, stronger treatment of indexes/query design/scaling and clearer free completion mechanics.

### Estruturas de Dados — 8.43 / 8.3

The 25-hour route covers the standard introductory family from lists/stacks/queues through heaps, hashing, search trees and graphs.

It is useful in pt-PT but cannot compete with MIT 6.006 on proof, algorithm analysis, problem-set transparency or breadth. Its marginal case is therefore weaker than the companion Algorithms course.

### Algoritmos e Complexidade — 8.56 / 8.6

The strongest of the three IPL theoretical-computing challengers for marginal coverage.

Its 25-hour syllabus reaches:

- asymptotic time/space reasoning;
- multiple sorting families;
- divide and conquer;
- dynamic programming;
- greedy strategy;
- sliding window;
- tree traversal;
- BFS/DFS/topological sorting/Dijkstra.

MIT 6.006 remains clearly superior intrinsically, but this course supplies a substantive European-Portuguese algorithms path absent from the canonical set.

## AI & Data / scientific practice

### Ciência Aberta e Dados FAIR — 8.44 / 8.7

The course is current and well targeted to a real professional/research need:

- FAIR;
- stewardship/curation;
- sensitive-data security;
- research-data policy;
- responsible AI in scientific work.

It does not solve the pt-PT technical machine-learning gap.

NASA Open Science 101 remains the stronger global open-science route because its workflow/tool practice is broader. This challenger is more valuable for Portuguese research-context accessibility than for intrinsic superiority.

## Natural Sciences

### Sustentabilidade Ambiental — 8.42 / 8.7

The course is broad rather than deep, but unusually strong in access:

- 27 hours;
- waste, water, energy, air and noise;
- tasks, quizzes and interactive activities;
- no specialist prerequisites;
- current page explicitly states a **fee-free certificate worth 1 ECTS**.

It should not be misrepresented as the missing climate-science anchor. Its role is Portuguese environmental/sustainability literacy.

## Writing & Communication

### Língua portuguesa em contexto de escrita académica — 8.54 / 8.8

A legitimate differentiated route rather than a language-diversity placeholder.

The 30-hour course covers:

- European-Portuguese register;
- formal oral/written communication;
- orthography and punctuation;
- paragraph design;
- academic style;
- task comprehension;
- summary, abstract and synthesis;
- explanatory/expository production.

The main limitation is the absence of verified personalised feedback. OpenLearn Essay and Report Writing remains stronger on universally free completion, but cannot substitute for Portuguese-language academic register.

## Languages

### Iniciação à Língua Portuguesa — 8.61 / 9.0

This is the strongest candidate in the batch by Recommendation.

University of Porto built a 32-hour, eight-unit beginner LMOOC specifically around **European Portuguese**, using professional audiovisual narrative and Porto-based everyday contexts.

The F2 edX model is a meaningful penalty: Audit users receive teaching content but not the formal graded/certificate path.

Unlike most category comparisons, stronger courses in German, French or Japanese do not make a Portuguese course redundant. They teach different target languages. The relevant admission question is therefore whether this is strong enough to create a European-Portuguese canonical slot, not whether it beats Nicos Weg on raw depth.

## Access note

The review uses the superseding Shallow Screening records from maintenance 11.

Five current NAU courses are conservatively F2 because, after NAU introduced partner-selectable paid assessment/certification in July 2026, their public pages do not course-specifically prove a zero-cost assessed completion path.

`nau-sustentabilidade-ambiental` remains F0 because its current page explicitly states fee-free certification and 1 ECTS.

The two PortugalX/edX routes are F2 because official NAU FAQ evidence states that Audit is free for course content while assessment/certification belong to Verified access.
