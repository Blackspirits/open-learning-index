# Deep Review scale batch 01 — volatile technology

## Purpose

This is the first scaled Deep Review batch after the mixed calibration pilot passed.

The batch deliberately prioritises the three highest-volatility technical areas in the current survivor pool:

- AI & Data
- Computer Science & Software
- Cybersecurity & IT

The objective is not to admit or rank courses yet. It is to apply the calibrated evidence model to direct competitors while current-route evidence is freshest.

## Head-to-head signals

### Agentic AI

`huggingface-agents-course` is the stronger default general recommendation.

It combines current agent concepts, multiple frameworks, hands-on work, a final challenge and a completely free certificate without mandatory paid cloud infrastructure.

`microsoft-learn-develop-ai-agents` remains a strong specialist choice for learners targeting Microsoft Foundry or Azure, but required Azure tooling and possible cloud consumption materially reduce portability and accessibility.

### Applied AI / ML

`huggingface-audio-course` remains unusually strong pedagogically and practically, but its 2023 stack cannot receive a currentness score comparable to living 2026 agent courses.

`mathworks-machine-learning-onramp` demonstrates why vendor specificity is not an automatic rejection: its browser-native exercises are excellent. The penalty belongs mainly in transferability/recommendation, not pedagogy.

`nau-inteligencia-artificial-machine-learning` earns recommendation value above its intrinsic Quality Score because it provides a low-friction, free credentialed pt-PT route. That language/access advantage does not justify inflating depth or practice.

### Programming / software

`berkeley-cs61a-fall-2026` is the strongest intrinsic course in this batch. Its public readings, videos, labs, homework and major projects are unusually complete and current.

Its Recommendation Score remains below its Quality Score because independent learners do not receive the enrolled-student submission, grading and staff-support infrastructure.

`odin-foundations` and `odin-full-stack-javascript` are stronger autonomous learning paths for learners whose goal is realistic developer workflow and portfolio practice.

`freecodecamp-javascript-v9` is the lowest-friction option and now has strong first-party evidence for its active certification design: interactive lessons, workshops, labs, reviews, quizzes, five required projects and an exam.

### Cybersecurity

`openlearn-intro-cyber-security` is the strongest general foundation in this batch: updated in March 2026, 24 hours long, broad, accessible and credentialed.

`microsoft-learn-ai-security-fundamentals` should be treated as a current specialist supplement, not a substitute. Its prompt-injection, jailbreak, data-exfiltration and red-team coverage is highly relevant, but two hours ten minutes cannot support foundation-level depth.

`ibm-skillsbuild-cybersecurity-fundamentals` sits between them: shorter than OpenLearn, broader than the Microsoft specialist path and attractive when a free industry credential matters.
