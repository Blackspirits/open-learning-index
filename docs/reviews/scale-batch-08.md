# Deep Review scale batch 08 — Education & Teaching

## Purpose

Close the current Phase 3 comparison family for Education & Teaching after correcting stale access and currency records.

The category now distinguishes three different forms of teacher development:

- broad pedagogy and online-teaching practice;
- focused classroom-methodology CPD;
- teacher-educator / professional-development leadership.

It also applies a stricter maintenance rule to digital education and cybersecurity than to stable teaching fundamentals.

## Pre-review maintenance

Two former `advance` records were removed from the scoring pool in #43.

### NAU — Competência digital docente: avaliação pedagógica

The historical course was strong: 27 hours, assessed tasks, rubrics and a free certificate.

However, NAU now labels it **Archived** and no current enrolment/completion route is visible.

Current decision: **reject**.

This is an availability decision, not a retrospective quality judgement.

### NAU / DGE — CiberSegurança nas Escolas

The learner route remains accessible, free and certified, but DGE identifies the underlying MOOC as originating in the **14 January–4 March 2019** run.

Because the course includes:

- cybersecurity;
- cybercrime;
- legislation;
- institutional security practice,

a 2019 evidence base cannot be treated as current without explicit revision evidence.

Current decision: **hold** pending currency verification.

## Head-to-head signals

### Broad teacher development

`openlearn-take-teaching-online` is the strongest broad Education candidate currently reviewed.

Its advantage comes from course design rather than institutional reputation.

The route provides:

- eight weeks;
- approximately 24 hours;
- weekly quizzes and activities;
- educator reflections;
- evidence/research references;
- inclusion and accessibility;
- online assessment;
- technology-selection decisions;
- free badge and Statement of Participation;
- 24 CPD points;
- extensive offline/download formats.

Its main weakness is currency.

The course was last formally updated in October 2022. Core online-teaching principles remain highly relevant, but named tools and parts of the digital-learning environment change faster than general pedagogy.

Quality therefore remains high at **9.04**, while Currency is explicitly lower than a current 2026 course.

### Classroom methodology — British Council family

The British Council TeachingEnglish survivors are not treated as interchangeable.

#### How to adapt resources

`british-council-how-adapt-resources` is the strongest new short CPD course in this batch.

Its three modules move from:

1. evaluating teaching resources;
2. selecting and adapting lesson plans;
3. using learners themselves as classroom resources.

The 2026–27 run is current, free, self-paced and includes a workbook and certificate.

Its **8.73 / 8.9** score reflects strong practical relevance but preserves a real Depth ceiling for a 9–12-hour course.

#### Communicative tasks

Already reviewed at **8.56 / 8.8**.

This remains a strong direct classroom-design option for genre, communicative activities and task-based learning.

#### Understanding language systems

Already reviewed at **8.49 / 8.7**.

Its role is subject/pedagogical knowledge around functional language, grammar and classroom presentation rather than general teaching development.

These courses can all remain useful in a later Phase 4 pass because they address distinct teacher needs. Provider concentration still needs to be considered when deciding how many British Council routes improve the final index.

### Teacher educators

`british-council-helping-teachers-learn` serves a different learner from the classroom-methodology courses.

Its three modules focus on:

- understanding teacher learning and training;
- facilitating collaborative learning and communities of practice;
- supporting teacher autonomy and self-directed professional development.

The 2025 workbook adds reflection and action planning.

At **8.64 / 8.8**, it is a strong specialist candidate, but the 9–12-hour scope remains a meaningful limit.

### Tricky topics

`openlearn-teaching-learning-tricky-topics` remains current `advance` with its calibration score of **8.43 / 8.0**.

Its practice-led pedagogical design remains useful, but its older 2019 evidence base reduces current recommendation. It is therefore not artificially rescored upward merely because newer Education reviews score strongly.

## Access and credential lessons

This category again shows that a certificate is not a Quality multiplier.

- British Council certificates improve completion evidence but do not turn 9–12-hour CPD into full qualifications.
- OpenLearn's badge, Statement of Participation and CPD recognition are useful, but its Quality comes from the 24-hour learning design and practice.
- The archived NAU certificate does not preserve current eligibility when the learner route disappears.
- An accessible certificate-bearing cybersecurity course can still be held when high-change content lacks verified currency.
