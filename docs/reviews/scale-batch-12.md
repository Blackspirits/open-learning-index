## Physics

### MIT 8.01SC Classical Mechanics

This is the strongest mechanics recommendation in the current Natural Sciences pool.

The OCW route was deliberately reorganized for independent study and contains:

- twelve weeks of structured lessons;
- online textbook readings;
- short topic videos;
- physical demonstrations;
- worked examples;
- twelve problem sets;
- calculus as a corequisite rather than a hidden requirement.

The Fall 2016 date creates almost no substantive currency penalty because the course is classical mechanics rather than frontier physics.

### Yale PHYS 200 and PHYS 201

The Yale sequence remains highly competitive despite its archive age.

PHYS 200 covers:

- mechanics;
- special relativity;
- gravitation;
- oscillations and waves;
- fluids;
- thermodynamics.

PHYS 201 continues through:

- electrostatics;
- circuits;
- magnetism;
- Maxwell's equations;
- optics;
- introductory quantum mechanics.

Both courses use weekly homework and formal examinations.

Their 2006-era recordings are not treated like old software or old biomedical guidance. The core physics remains standard undergraduate material.

MIT 8.01 wins for a mechanics-specific independent-study experience; Yale provides a broader two-course physics sequence.

## General chemistry

### MIT 5.111SC

MIT's Principles of Chemical Science is the highest-quality general chemistry package in this pool.

It includes:

- lecture videos and notes;
- conceptual clicker questions;
- problem sets and solutions;
- practice exams;
- four term exams;
- final exam;
- complete solutions.

The course dates to Fall 2014, but atomic structure, bonding, thermodynamics, equilibrium, acid-base chemistry and kinetics remain stable enough that age is only a modest penalty.

### Khan AP/College Chemistry

Khan is the strongest low-friction mastery alternative:

- 9 units;
- 66 tracked skills;
- quizzes;
- unit tests;
- cumulative course challenge.

It is the better default for learners who need continuous feedback and adaptive practice rather than a traditional university lecture/problem-set experience.

### Saylor CHEM101

Saylor provides the strongest current certificate-bearing route:

- 73 hours;
- free enrolment;
- auto-graded assessments;
- free final exam;
- free completion certificate;
- syllabus last modified 26 September 2025.

The optional ACE-credit proctoring fee is kept separate from the free course.

### MIT 7.016

MIT remains stronger for academic depth and university-style problem solving.

The Fall 2018 course contains:

- lectures;
- recitations;
- six problem sets with solutions;
- three exams with solutions;
- molecular biology, genetics, cell biology and biochemistry.

Unlike classical mechanics, molecular and cellular biology changes meaningfully over time. The core remains excellent, but current genomics, methods and newer biological findings require supplementation.

### Saylor BIO101

Saylor offers a current structured alternative:

- 36 hours;
- auto-graded assessment;
- final exam;
- free certificate;
- syllabus last modified 26 September 2025.

It ranks below MIT on depth and below Khan on feedback design, but provides an unusually accessible current credential-bearing route.

## Organic chemistry

Yale CHEM 125a and 125b remain strong because the courses teach organic chemistry through evidence, models, energy and mechanism rather than reaction memorisation.

The sequence includes:

- molecular structure;
- orbitals and bonding;
- stereochemistry;
- conformational analysis;
- reactivity;
- kinetics;
- substitution;
- spectroscopy;
- synthesis;
- oxidation/reduction;
- multiple midterms and final examinations.

Archive age does matter for modern reagents, synthetic methods and instrumentation, but not enough to invalidate the core mechanistic reasoning.

These are therefore high-Quality archive courses with slightly lower Recommendation than the strongest current foundation routes.

## Climate-related routes

### FAO Introduction to climate-smart agriculture

FAO's course is technically credible and extremely accessible, but short.

It contains four lessons totaling roughly 90 minutes:

1. climate change and global warming;
2. impacts on agriculture and food security;
3. adaptation and mitigation;
4. climate-smart agriculture and its five-step process.

The broader FAO Climate-Smart Agriculture series documents interactive exercises, final tests and digital badge certification.

The main limitation is currentness transparency. The public course page exposes no reliable recent revision date, which matters in climate adaptation and agricultural practice.

### EU Climate Action Academy

The EU Academy route is exceptionally current:

- last published **9 September 2026**;
- citizen engagement;
- climate communication;
- community mobilisation;
- citizen science;
- youth leadership;
- AI for climate solutions;
- EU climate policy;
- climate disinformation and misinformation;
- participation certificate.

The scoring is intentionally restrained on Depth and Practice because this is principally a climate-action, communication and policy academy rather than a deep climate-science curriculum.

## Currency rules reinforced

This batch confirms several subject-sensitive rules:

1. **Archive age is not a universal penalty.**
   - Classical mechanics from 2006/2016 remains highly current conceptually.
   - Foundational electromagnetism and organic chemistry remain valid.

2. **Empirical sciences require stronger recency scrutiny.**
   - MIT Biology 2018 remains excellent but cannot be treated as fully current on modern genomics and methods.

3. **A current platform is not automatically a current course.**
   - FAO's course remains live, but unclear revision history lowers Currency.

4. **Currentness does not equal scientific depth.**
   - EU Climate Action Academy is extremely current yet action/policy-oriented.

5. **Cross-disciplinary scientific-practice courses must be labelled honestly.**
   - NASA Open Science 101 scores highly for research practice, not for disciplinary science coverage.

6. **Credentials do not inflate Quality.**
   - MIT/Yale F2 archives can outperform certificate-bearing routes when pedagogy, depth and practice are stronger.
