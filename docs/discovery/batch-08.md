# Discovery Batch 08 — corrective language micro-wave

**Date:** 2026-09-12

## Scope

This is the single corrective micro-discovery pass authorised by the final v0.2 saturation audit. It is deliberately **not** a broad discovery wave.

The audit identified two concrete language-learning gaps after full screening and hold resolution:

- no surviving complete Arabic pathway;
- no surviving complete Mandarin Chinese pathway.

The approved cap was 8–12 new candidates, with permission to stop earlier as soon as each gap had serious challengers and marginal search quality declined.

## Outcome

The search stopped after **4 new candidates** because the gap now has meaningful competition without creating another screening backlog.

| Target | Candidate | Provider | Provisional access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandarin Chinese | 初级汉语口语入门 | Beijing Language and Culture University / UMOOCs | F1 |
| Mandarin Chinese | Mandarin Chinese Level 1 | MandarinX / edX | F2 |
| Arabic | Arabic Grammar Course | Madinah Arabic | F1 |
| Arabic | General Language learning pathway | Al Jazeera Learning Arabic | F1 |

All four were shallow-screened immediately in Shallow 12; there is no new unscreened debt.

## Mandarin Chinese findings

### Added — BLCU / UMOOCs: 初级汉语口语入门

The current 15th run is explicitly free and open from 24 August to 27 December 2026. It is designed for zero-beginner learners and exposes:

- 18 weeks of access;
- 32 videos;
- 10 assessments;
- 32 downloadable resources;
- pinyin plus practical communication around greetings, family, location, transport, shopping, food, hobbies, weather and travel.

This is materially stronger than the short extracts and tasters previously found.

### Added — MandarinX / edX: Mandarin Chinese Level 1

MandarinX's current edX provider page explicitly states that its courses can be audited for free. Level 1 provides a self-paced six-week beginner route with English mediation, everyday vocabulary, tones and practical communication.

The verified certificate and some verified-track enrichment are paid, so the free learner route is conservatively F2 rather than F0/F1.

### Researched but not added

- **OpenLearn — Beginners' Chinese: a taster course:** explicitly a taster, not a complete course.
- **OpenLearn — Getting started with Chinese 1:** an eight-hour extract from a fuller course; too partial for the identified gap.
- **Peking University / Coursera — Chinese for Beginners:** substantial academically, but the current Coursera public route exposes preview/trial mechanics rather than a clearly complete free path; no candidate was created without explicit full-free evidence.
- **FutureLearn beginner Chinese offerings:** free access is time-limited or trial-based and the surfaced courses are too introductory to beat the selected challengers.
- **BLCU — 初级综合汉语:** pedagogically strong but the surfaced comprehensive run is archived; a current active free learner run was not confirmed.
- **BLCU — 初级汉语语法:** current and free, but it is a grammar specialist from the same provider; once a stronger communicative BLCU course and an independent self-paced comparator were admitted, adding another same-provider specialist would create avoidable micro-wave redundancy.

## Arabic findings

### Added — Madinah Arabic: Arabic Grammar Course

The site exposes more than 90 sequenced free grammar and morphology lessons with audio-supported examples and interactive exercises. Learners who cannot read Arabic are explicitly directed to a separate free Arabic Reading Course first.

The course is strongest for Modern Standard Arabic grammar, reading and morphology rather than balanced four-skill communication. Some examples overlap Classical/Qur'anic Arabic; this is recorded for deep comparison rather than hidden.

### Added — Al Jazeera Learning Arabic: General Language pathway

Al Jazeera describes the site as a **free open educational source**. Its General Language learning area is a broad levelled pathway spanning introductory through proficiency material and includes:

- an Arabic letters-and-sounds sequence;
- beginner and elementary daily-life lessons;
- grammar series;
- intermediate through proficiency texts;
- interactive exercises with results;
- placement testing;
- mediator-language support.

It is a non-linear open-learning pathway rather than a conventional cohort course. This remains eligible because the Open Learning Index explicitly evaluates open learning experiences and resources, not courses only.

### Researched but not added

- **King Salman Global Academy — Ahlan Wa Sahlan:** very strong structured Arabic programme, but the current provider FAQ describes the free option as a subscription/trial before paid plans; this fails the project's full-free requirement.
- **OpenLearn — Introduction to Arabic:** current and genuinely free, but explicitly a four-hour two-week pre-course centred on alphabet and writing foundations; too partial to close the gap.
- **ArabicOnline.eu:** substantial Modern Standard Arabic programme, but the complete beginner-to-intermediate route is subscription-funded; the free material is sample/introductory content only.
- **FSI Modern Written Arabic archives:** extremely substantial and free, but old archive material is primarily reading/press-oriented and less pedagogically usable/current than the selected challengers.

## Saturation decision

The micro-wave stops at **4 candidates**, well below the 8–12 hard cap.

Reasons:

1. Arabic now has two materially different serious challengers: a deep structured grammar route and a broad interactive levelled pathway.
2. Mandarin now has a current assessed university beginner route and a self-paced English-mediated free-audit comparator.
3. Additional search results were increasingly partial, trial-based, archived or redundant.
4. Optional History/Humanities discovery is not justified merely because unused candidate capacity remains.

No further v0.2 discovery should occur after this batch unless a blocker is discovered in the newly admitted language candidates during validation.
