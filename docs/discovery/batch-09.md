# Targeted maintenance discovery batch 09 — challenger scan 01

## Purpose

This is the first challenger scan after v0.5 publication readiness.

It is **not** a restart of broad discovery. Every candidate below must address a coverage weakness already documented by Phase 4/5 or plausibly displace an incumbent.

The scan deliberately stops at four candidates rather than filling a quota.

## Why these four clear Discovery

### MIT 6.7220J Nonlinear Optimization

The Mathematics admission pass explicitly left a broad optimisation gap because the two-hour, MATLAB-specific MathWorks Onramp was too narrow.

MIT's Spring 2025 course is materially different:

- graduate-level nonlinear optimisation;
- convex and nonconvex optimality;
- constrained and unconstrained methods;
- first- and second-order methods;
- Lagrange multipliers, convex analysis and duality;
- six problem sets with solutions;
- exam material and solutions;
- applications including machine learning, control and resource allocation.

Official evidence:
- https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-7220j-nonlinear-optimization-spring-2025/
- https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-7220j-nonlinear-optimization-spring-2025/pages/syllabus/

The high prerequisites are a recommendation constraint, not a Discovery blocker.

### MIT 12.340x Global Warming Science

Natural Sciences deliberately published no climate anchor because the reviewed FAO course was too short and the EU Climate Action Academy was policy/action-oriented rather than disciplinary climate science.

MIT 12.340x provides:

- undergraduate climate-system science;
- radiative/energy balance;
- greenhouse-gas physics;
- atmospheric and ocean circulation;
- aerosols, orbital effects and climate evidence;
- climate models;
- lectures, problem sets and exams;
- free access through MIT OpenCourseWare/Open Learning Library.

Official evidence:
- https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-340x-global-warming-science-spring-2020/

Its 2020 teaching date is material. Shallow Screening must determine whether that age is acceptable for a best-current climate-science recommendation rather than assuming stable fundamentals solve the currentness problem.

### Saylor BUS633 Sales Management

Marketing & Sales deliberately kept a dedicated sales gap rather than promote HubSpot Inbound Sales at only 3h06.

BUS633 is a much stronger challenger on paper:

- 29 expected hours;
- end-to-end selling process;
- consultative selling and customer needs;
- follow-up, loyalty and sales management;
- CRM, prospecting, outreach, scheduling, chat and reporting tools;
- final assessment;
- free completion certificate.

Official evidence:
- https://learn.saylor.org/course/bus633
- https://learn.saylor.org/mod/page/view.php?id=83120

Its graduate/master's alignment must not be conflated with free graduate credit.

### Microsoft AI Red Teaming 101

Cybersecurity & IT records AI security as an explicit high-change gap because the earlier Microsoft AI Security Fundamentals route was only about two hours.

The current AI Red Teaming series adds a substantially more specialised practice layer:

- direct and indirect prompt injection;
- single- and multi-turn attacks;
- defensive mitigations;
- real-world cases;
- automated adversarial testing with PyRIT;
- hands-on demonstrations;
- linked Microsoft red-teaming labs.

Official evidence:
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security/ai-red-team/training

Shallow Screening must verify the practical route, learner friction and whether the labs are sufficiently accessible to justify F1.

## Searches that did not produce a candidate

The scan also revisited other documented gaps and deliberately added nothing where the evidence did not clear the Discovery bar.

### Vendor-neutral electronics / embedded systems

No current, deep, freely accessible vendor-neutral pathway with reproducible labs was found.

OpenLearn's electronics introduction is useful but too old and shallow for the specific gap. UT Austin's Shape the World MOOCs are currently described by their instructor as dormant because the required boards/compilers are difficult to source.

**Decision:** keep the gap open.

### pt-PT AI / machine learning

Current NAU routes remain useful for accessibility, but the deeper technical gap identified in Phase 4 is not resolved by another short literacy/education-focused course.

**Decision:** keep the gap open.

### pt-PT legal education

Current short RGPD/digital-rights material does not yet provide the depth needed to challenge the canonical legal set. Cohort/tutor programmes aimed at legal professionals were not treated as general open self-study courses without access evidence.

**Decision:** keep the gap open.

### Current history of epidemics including COVID-19

A current university course with the right academic scope was found in conventional enrolment, not as a genuinely open free online route. A free public-health mini-course was too short to solve the documented History & Culture gap.

**Decision:** keep the gap open.
