## Access uncertainty

Discovery deliberately records all three as `UNVERIFIED`.

NAU states in June 2026 that its cybersecurity catalogue disseminates accessible, massive and free cybersecurity content. However, from 6 July 2026, partners can select among:

- fully free courses;
- free/open teaching with paid assessment/certification;
- paid advanced training.

Therefore Shallow Screening must verify the exact current learner journey for each course rather than infer F0 from the presence of assessment/certificate text.

## Searches that did not produce a current candidate

### Deep technical AI / machine learning

Several strong pt-PT options exist but are not currently open general-learning routes:

- Técnico Lisboa's 40-hour DataScience: KDD Process and DataScience: Classification editions ended 31 August 2026 after enrolment closed 24 August;
- CESAE Digital has substantial 75–100h free AI/ML training, but current cohorts are full or eligibility-restricted;
- IEFP exposes 100h distance AI programmes, but access is cohort-, employment-status- and/or location-restricted rather than a generally open MOOC;
- NAU's new DSPA Generative AI certification is only a 4h preparatory route and its certification exam is paid.

**Decision:** keep the deep open pt-PT AI/ML gap explicit. Monitor new Técnico/CESAE/IEFP editions but do not represent restricted cohorts as open courses.

### Finance

Todos Contam remains authoritative and active, but the currentness blocker is not resolved. Search still exposes supporting investment/credit materials tied to old regulatory documents, including CMVM material from the 2012–2015 period.

NAU's 12-hour Bitcoin course is active but originated in 2022 and operates in a high-change financial/crypto domain without a verified substantive update.

**Decision:** add no new finance candidate. Keep Todos Contam on hold rather than lower the currency standard.

### Foreign-language learning through pt-PT

Universidade Aberta's 16-hour four-language sampler was already rejected for insufficient depth.

Cambridge English provides a large current free practice library with a Portuguese-facing site, but it is an activity collection rather than a coherent pt-PT-taught course/pathway. British Council's structured online courses are paid beyond trials.

**Decision:** no new course candidate.

### European Portuguese A2/B1+

Strong higher-level routes exist but are paid or conventional enrolment:

- Camões A2 self-study: €180;
- Camões A2 tutored: €250;
- FLUL/O Meu Português A2: commercial paid route;
- current university A2/B1/B2 courses are conventional paid/cohort courses.

**Decision:** keep the free/open A2/B1+ European-Portuguese gap explicit.
