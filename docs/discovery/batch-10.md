# Targeted maintenance discovery batch 10 — pt-PT challenger scan 01

## Purpose

This maintenance micro-wave responds to a real publication weakness: only a very small part of the canonical set is currently delivered in European Portuguese.

It does **not** lower the quality bar for language representation. The scan targets serious pt-PT learning routes across the documented areas of interest: programming/computer science, AI/data, natural sciences, finance, law/public policy, professional skills, writing/communication and language learning.

Only candidates with a plausible role against the existing index are added. Discovery remains research intake, not endorsement.

## Why these candidates clear Discovery

### Programming and computer science

The four technical NAU courses form the first substantial pt-PT computer-science family found in maintenance research rather than a collection of two-to-eight-hour digital-literacy primers.

- **Introdução à Programação: Conceitos Fundamentais** provides 60 hours of structured algorithmic problem solving, flowcharts and pseudocode with automatically evaluated exercises.
- **Fundamentos de Bases de Dados** covers relational design from conceptual to physical models, SQL, normalization, PostgreSQL and Python integration.
- **Estruturas de Dados** covers the standard progression from lists, stacks and queues through heaps, hash tables, trees and graphs.
- **Algoritmos e Complexidade** covers time/space complexity, classical sorting, divide and conquer, dynamic programming, greedy techniques and graph algorithms.

Provider concentration is deliberately not resolved at Discovery. They are distinct curricular roles from the same Portuguese polytechnic ecosystem and must later compete on quality, completeness and marginal value.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/introducao-a-programacao-conceitos-fundamentais/
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/fundamentos-de-bases-de-dados/
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/estruturas-de-dados/
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/algoritmos-e-complexidade/

### AI/data and scientific data practice

**Ciência Aberta e Dados FAIR** is not a substitute for a deep machine-learning course. It is a current 20-hour research-data course covering open science, FAIR principles, stewardship/curation, sensitive-data security, policy and responsible AI in scientific work. Six knowledge checks and a final exam give it enough structure to warrant comparison.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/ciencia-aberta-e-dados-fair-principios-praticas-e-politicas/

The deeper technical **pt-PT AI/ML gap remains open**. The strongest technical 2026 find, Técnico Lisboa's 40-hour *Machine Learning, Maths & Ethics: Hands-on*, had already closed enrolment on 23 June 2026 and ended on 30 June 2026. Existing NAU AI introductions have already been reviewed by this project and were too shallow to enter the canonical set. A future Técnico edition should be monitored rather than represented as currently accessible.

### Natural sciences

**Sustentabilidade Ambiental - Mobilizar, Observar e Operacionalizar** is a 27-hour environmental course spanning waste, water, energy, air quality and noise. It includes tasks, quizzes and interactive activities. The official page explicitly states that successful completion gives a fee-free certificate from the Escola Superior de Saúde de Lisboa corresponding to 1 ECTS.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/sustentabilidade-ambiental/

This is a materially stronger signal than short awareness material, but Deep Review must still judge scientific depth and currentness.

### Finance

**Todos Contam** is the official e-learning route of Portugal's financial-education programme backed by the national financial supervisors. It exposes a broad sequence covering household budgets, payments, saving and investment, credit, insurance, economic/financial-system basics and digital finance.

Official evidence:
- https://elearning.todoscontam.pt/

It is entered conservatively as F2. The public route does not show a general completion credential, and some financial/legal material may be date-sensitive. Shallow Screening must audit lesson currency rather than assuming regulator ownership makes every page current.

### Professional skills

**Liderança, Gestão e Valorização de Pessoas** offers 39 hours of pt-PT instruction across HR, career reflection, emotional intelligence, leadership, team management and competencies, with interactive activities, module quizzes and a certificate threshold.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/lideranca-gestao-e-valorizacao-de-pessoas/

**Língua portuguesa em contexto de escrita académica** provides a separate productive-communication role: 30 hours focused on the European-Portuguese variant, formal oral/written communication, paragraph structure, summaries, synthesis and expository/explanatory writing, with assessed completion.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/lingua-portuguesa-em-contexto-de-escrita-academica/

A 25-hour DISC-centred communication course was researched but deliberately not added. Duration alone does not justify Discovery when the central behavioural framework raises a material evidence-quality question.

### Language learning

**Iniciação à Língua Portuguesa** from the University of Porto is the strongest new language-learning find. The official page explicitly states **Português europeu**, requires no prior knowledge, runs across eight practical units and 32 hours, and requires a passing quiz in every unit for certification.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/iniciacao-a-lingua-portuguesa/

**Línguas Vivas Estrangeiras** from Universidade Aberta is permanently open and gives Portuguese-speaking learners a bridge into German, Spanish and French at introductory level plus English practice around A2/B1.

Official evidence:
- https://aulaberta.uab.pt/blocks/catalog/detail.php?id=5

It is only 16 hours shared across four languages. That weakness is explicit: Shallow Screening must reject it if it functions merely as a promotional sampler rather than a substantively useful learning experience.

### Law and public policy

No deep, broad, current pt-PT law MOOC surfaced.

The specialist **Cidadãos Estrangeiros: Trabalho e Formação** is nevertheless worth testing because it addresses a concrete Portugal-specific learner need: employment access, training, qualification recognition and certification, with formal module tests and a participation certificate.

Official evidence:
- https://www.nau.edu.pt/pt/curso/cidadaos-estrangeiros-trabalho-e-formacao/

Its seven-hour duration and fast-changing procedural/legal context are material constraints. It is not treated as closing the project's general pt-PT legal-education gap.

## Searches that did not produce a current candidate

### Deep technical AI / machine learning

Técnico Lisboa's 40-hour *Machine Learning, Maths & Ethics: Hands-on* is much stronger technically than the short NAU literacy courses, but its 2026 enrolment closed on 23 June and the course ended on 30 June. The same availability problem affected other substantial Técnico data-science MOOCs inspected.

**Decision:** do not add an inaccessible course; monitor for the next edition.

### Broad pt-PT legal education

Short RGPD/digital-rights courses remain too shallow, while deeper university legal training located in the scan is paid, cohort-restricted or not evidenced as an open free learner route.

**Decision:** keep the broad law gap open; test only the specialist IEFP course above.

### Full foreign-language pathways taught through pt-PT

The Universidade Aberta sampler is the only current open university route found that clearly bridges pt-PT learners into several foreign languages. Its 16-hour shared scope is not comparable to a complete A1/A2 pathway.

**Decision:** admit it to Discovery only so completeness can be tested; do not claim the foreign-language gap is solved.
