## Key decisions

### Learn European Portuguese — admit

Languages represent different target-language needs. A German, French or Japanese category leader cannot substitute for Portuguese.

The University of Porto route is substantial enough to stand independently:

- about 32 hours;
- eight narrative units;
- explicit European Portuguese;
- everyday communication, pronunciation, vocabulary and grammar;
- professional audiovisual production and cultural context.

The edX Audit model is a real F2 limitation and remains visible. It does not erase the target-language coverage value.

### Academic writing in pt-PT — admit

OpenLearn remains the broader fully free general academic-writing default.

The ISCTE course adds a non-substitutable language/register role:

- European-Portuguese conventions;
- formal academic style;
- paragraph construction;
- summary, abstract and synthesis;
- explanatory/expository production.

This is productive writing instruction, not token representation.

### Programming — admit one beginner route and one algorithms route

The canonical Computer Science set is already strong, so pt-PT candidates must add a real pathway rather than duplicate incumbents.

`Introducao a Programacao` earns a slot because its 60-hour algorithmic/pseudocode progression is substantial and beginner-oriented.

Among the three adjacent IPL candidates, only `Algoritmos e Complexidade` is admitted. It has the strongest distinct scope and gives Portuguese learners an intermediate path into algorithm design and complexity.

`Fundamentos de Bases de Dados` and `Estruturas de Dados` remain useful reviewed alternatives but are not promoted.

### Sustainability — admit without pretending it is climate science

The project still lacks a deep current climate-science anchor.

Sustentabilidade Ambiental is admitted for a different need: accessible broad environmental literacy in pt-PT, with 27 hours, interactive assessment and an explicitly fee-free 1-ECTS completion route.

The climate-science gap therefore remains open.

### FAIR data — do not admit

The course is current and useful, but NASA Open Science 101 is stronger in workflow/tool practice and free completion.

More importantly, admitting FAIR data as the Portuguese AI/Data answer would obscure the actual unresolved gap: **deep technical AI/ML in pt-PT**.

## Provider concentration

This batch deliberately rejects catalogue mirroring.

Instituto Politecnico de Lisboa has three adjacent computing candidates. Only `Algoritmos e Complexidade` is admitted from that cluster. Its separate Sustainability course is admitted in Natural Sciences because it serves a genuinely different learning need.

Still open:

- deep technical AI / machine learning;

- finance, pending currentness resolution for Todos Contam;
- deeper foreign-language pathways taught through pt-PT;

Those are future challenger targets, not reasons to lower today's admission threshold.
