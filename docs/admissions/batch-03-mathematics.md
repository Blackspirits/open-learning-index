## Statistics family

Phase 4 deliberately avoids publishing every strong introductory statistics alternative.

- **MIT 18.05** becomes the primary rigorous all-round route.
- **Harvard Stat 110** is retained because probability depth is a distinct learning need, not a duplicate.
- **Khan Academy** is retained because its basic-algebra entry point, mastery tracking and very low friction serve a materially different learner.
- The two CMU OLI courses remain valuable reviewed alternatives, but do not add enough marginal value to the compact canonical set.

This is a direct example of Phase 4 doing work that scores alone cannot do: CMU Probability & Statistics scores 9.10 / 9.2 and still does not enter.

## MIT concentration

After this batch, Mathematics & Statistics is heavily represented by MIT.

That concentration is accepted only where the learning roles are genuinely distinct:

- single-variable calculus;
- multivariable calculus;
- differential equations;
- linear algebra;
- probability/statistics;
- discrete mathematics for computer science;
- real analysis.

The batch explicitly stops before admitting every excellent MIT specialist. Complex Variables, Theory of Numbers and Statistics for Applications are preserved as Deep-Reviewed resources but fail the higher marginal-value threshold created by provider concentration and specialist scope.
