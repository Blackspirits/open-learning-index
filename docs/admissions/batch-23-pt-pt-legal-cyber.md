## Regulação Digital — admit

Eight hours is compact, so this does not become a general law anchor.

It is admitted because its ten-module scope is unusually current and distinct:

- European digital-regulation context;
- platforms and digital markets;
- data regulation;
- AI and emerging technologies;
- content moderation and e-commerce;
- digital accessibility;
- compliance tools and implementation.

Council of Europe HELP remains stronger in specific privacy and AI/human-rights law roles. Regulação Digital complements those courses with a broader current digital-policy surface and full pt-PT instruction.

## Responsável de Cibersegurança — admit

This course directly solves the problem that caused older Portuguese cybersecurity/legal courses to be rejected.

The new route is current with Portugal's 2025/2026 cybersecurity regime and covers:

- law and standards;
- risk;
- organisational controls;
- audit;
- asset inventories;
- incidents;
- policy and planning;
- annual reporting and compliance.

Cisco Cyber Threat Management remains stronger for labs and credential mechanics. This course is stronger for Portuguese organisational/legal context.

## Cibersegurança para Executivos: Preparação para a NIS2 — admit

At 30 hours, this is a substantial executive course rather than a brief awareness module.

Its role is organisational leadership:

- NIS/NIS2;
- continuity and incident response;
- supply-chain security;
- secure acquisition/development/maintenance;
- vulnerability management;
- cryptography;
- access controls/MFA;
- cyber-hygiene.

It can coexist with Responsável de Cibersegurança because the learner roles differ: operational cybersecurity responsibility versus executive governance and decision-making.

## Access

All three are published conservatively as **F2**.

The instructional content is available through NAU's free Audit mode, but the current course-specific cost of assessment/certification is not explicitly established after the July 2026 platform payment change.

If later verification proves a fully free assessed route, these records can be upgraded without changing their admission rationale.
