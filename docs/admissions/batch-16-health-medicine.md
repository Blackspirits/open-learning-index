## Editorial principles

### Clinical-research roles are complementary, not interchangeable

The admitted clinical-research pathway separates:

- WHO Good Practices — broad current scientific/ethical trial principles;
- TGHN GCP — ICH E6(R3) compliance;
- TGHN Research Ethics — participant/community ethical reasoning;
- TGHN Data Life Cycle — research-data governance and sharing.

These are distinct professional competencies rather than four versions of one course.

### Provider authority does not override depth

WHO Chemical Hazards Part I is not admitted simply because WHO is authoritative.

Part II is still on hold and Part I is too limited to represent a complete chemical-hazards pathway alone.

### Very short professional modules remain supplements

FAO Basic Concepts and TGHN GCLP both have valid professional uses, but neither provides enough sustained learning for the canonical core.

### Nutrition coverage is retained only where it has real professional substance

FAO Improving nutrition through agriculture and food systems is admitted because it uses realistic scenarios and connects policy/programme decisions to nutrition outcomes.

Its currentness score is weaker than the clinical-research leaders, so it needs closer monitoring.

## Coverage gap

Chemical-hazard readiness remains unresolved because the complete WHO series is not yet publicly auditable enough for Phase 4.

This is a targeted evidence/coverage gap, not a reason to lower the bar.
