## Editorial principles

### Legal currentness is a gate, not a bonus

Recent course launch or cohort activity is not accepted as evidence that legal content is current.

That rule keeps the journalist-safety HELP course on hold and supports the three admitted CoE courses only where explicit 2025/2026 revision evidence exists.

### Same-provider specialists can coexist when the law is genuinely different

Three Council of Europe HELP courses are admitted, but their learning roles are distinct:

- AI/human-rights governance;
- cybercrime and electronic evidence;
- data protection/privacy.

This is not catalogue mirroring: each covers a separate high-change legal domain and would be difficult to substitute with the others.

### General foundations beat overlapping applied variants

WIPO DL-101 wins the core IP slot because it is substantially deeper, broader and assessed.

IP Panorama remains a useful business-facing specialist but does not add enough marginal value for a second WIPO core slot.

### Language access cannot erase a depth gap

`nau-direitos-deveres-digital` is valuable for pt-PT access but remains a four-hour literacy course.

The resulting lack of a top-tier pt-PT legal course is recorded as a **quality/access blind spot** eligible for targeted future discovery.
