## Editorial principles

### Intrinsic quality and current recommendation can diverge sharply

MIT 9.00SC has the highest candidate Quality score in the family.

It is still not admitted.

Its self-checks, solved exams and independent-study design are excellent, but psychology has changed enough since 2011 that the canonical core does not need a second old general archive when Yale already serves that role and Saylor offers a current assessed path.

### Current general foundation + archive can coexist

Saylor does not erase Yale.

The pair serves different needs:

- Saylor — current, structured, assessed and credential-bearing;
- Yale — engaging high-quality lecture archive with older evidence.

Phase 4 therefore records Saylor as a complement/current default rather than mechanically deleting the reference fixture.

### Short courses can earn admission when the role is genuinely distinct

Psychology around the world is only six hours, but it provides current cross-cultural perspective and directly addresses the limits of WEIRD generalisation.

Its value is not breadth; it is correcting a blind spot in broader psychology curricula.

### Mental-health literacy is not clinical training

The NAU course is admitted for current pt-PT mental-health literacy and first-response awareness in higher education.

The canonical description must not imply diagnosis, treatment or professional clinical competence.
