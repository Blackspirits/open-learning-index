## Optimisation — admit despite MIT concentration

Mathematics was already the most MIT-concentrated category and Phase 4 deliberately rejected excellent MIT specialists where they added too little marginal value.

6.7220J is different.

The repository explicitly left **broad optimisation** open. The only reviewed specialist was MathWorks Optimization Onramp, whose two-hour MATLAB-specific scope was insufficient as the canonical answer.

6.7220J adds:

- a Spring 2025 curriculum;
- convex and nonconvex optimisation;
- constrained and unconstrained methods;
- first- and second-order algorithms;
- Lagrangian/duality theory;
- six problem sets with solutions;
- exam material;
- modern ML/control/resource-allocation applications.

It therefore earns admission despite becoming an eighth MIT mathematics slot.

That exception should make later MIT admissions **harder**, not easier: another MIT course now needs unusually strong marginal value.

## Climate science — keep the gap visible

MIT Global Warming Science is not rejected for low quality.

At **9.25 Quality**, it is stronger than many canonical courses.

The problem is role-specific currentness.

A 2026 climate-science anchor should not stop at a Spring 2020 empirical/assessment context, before the IPCC AR6 cycle and later observational evidence. The physical foundations remain excellent, but publishing it as the current best answer would undermine the index's own currentness standard.

Decision: **do_not_admit**.

The climate-science gap remains open for a current full curriculum.

## Sales — admit on depth, not on coverage pressure

Phase 4 previously refused to admit HubSpot Inbound Sales merely because sales was uncovered.

BUS633 now clears the bar independently:

- 29 hours versus 3h06;
- ten coherent units;
- full selling process;
- CRM/digital-sales context;
- unit assessments;
- repeatable practice exam;
- free final exam;
- 70% passing threshold;
- free certificate;
- October 2025 syllabus revision.

It complements rather than duplicates:

- `saylor-bus203-marketing` — broad marketing;
- `hubspot-revenue-operations` — cross-functional operations.

Decision: **admit**.

## AI security — current is not enough

Microsoft AI Red Teaming 101 improves substantially on the previously rejected AI Security Fundamentals path.

The content is excellent for a specialist:

- prompt injection;
- multi-turn attacks;
- mitigations;
- PyRIT automation;
- production case studies;
- open challenge/notebook repository.

But full lab execution requires Azure OpenAI or OpenAI API credentials.

For an AI-security course, practical adversarial testing is central rather than optional decoration. The free route therefore does not yet deliver enough reproducible practice to justify a canonical slot.

Decision: **do_not_admit**.

The AI-security gap remains open.
