## Editorial principles

### Target-language diversity is real coverage, not catalogue duplication

Three Language Transfer courses are admitted because they cover Spanish, Greek and Swahili at a quality level that independently clears the bar.

They share a method, but one language cannot substitute for another.

The limitation is explicitly recorded: Language Transfer is strongest on structural/oral reasoning and must be supplemented for reading, writing, vocabulary breadth and authentic interaction.

### Complete pathways deserve extra weight

`dw-nicos-weg-a1-b1` is a category leader because it spans A1 through B1 with a coherent narrative and dense integrated practice.

Minato and TV5MONDE are narrower in level, but both have exceptionally strong beginner-course design.

### Sequences can justify two records

freeCodeCamp A2 and B1 English for Developers are retained as one coherent professional-English progression.

The Beta label and exam mechanics require closer monitoring than stable language archives, so both receive short review intervals.

### Stronger free route beats easier audit

BLCU is harder to navigate for international zero-beginners, but its 18-week assessed Mandarin route is substantially stronger than MandarinX's free audit.

Phase 4 does not trade away major depth/practice advantages merely for a friendlier paid-platform interface.

### Specialist grammar does not require a second Arabic core slot

Al Jazeera already provides the stronger broad Arabic ecosystem.

Madinah Arabic remains a useful specialist grammar supplement without needing a separate canonical slot.
