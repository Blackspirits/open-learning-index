## Provider-concentration policy

Seven of the eight current survivors are HubSpot courses.

Phase 3 intentionally did not distort their scores to compensate for this.

Phase 4 now applies the correct filter: multiple HubSpot courses survive only where each adds a genuinely distinct learning role.

The existing canonical **HubSpot Digital Marketing** record remains the broad practical digital-marketing reference.

### HubSpot Revenue Operations — admit

Revenue Operations is not merely another acquisition channel.

Its nearly seven-hour route covers:

- process friction;
- accountability;
- SLAs;
- team design;
- cross-functional alignment;
- iterative RevOps strategy.

That role spans marketing, sales and customer success and is not served by the existing Digital Marketing course.

Provider concentration is therefore accepted as a justified exception.

### HubSpot AEO Fundamentals — admit

AEO Fundamentals has only **8.39 Quality** because its 2h20 scope and practice depth are limited.

Its **9.0 Recommendation** is still defensible because Currency is **10.0** and the course covers a current learning need absent from the canonical set:

- AI-search visibility;
- answer engines;
- AI citations;
- E-E-A-T in AI discovery;
- structured data;
- measurement of emerging search behaviour.

This is precisely why Quality and Recommendation remain separate.

The course receives a **90-day review interval** because this content can age quickly.

### Saylor BUS203 — admit

Saylor BUS203 improves the category in a different way.

It is the strongest broad general-marketing foundation in the current survivor pool:

- 30 hours;
- seven units;
- segmentation and targeting;
- research;
- product strategy;
- pricing;
- supply chains and distribution;
- promotion and campaigns;
- repeatable unit assessments;
- free final exam and completion certificate.

Its syllabus was last modified in October 2025.

It complements, rather than duplicates, HubSpot Digital Marketing and materially improves provider diversity.

## Strong non-admissions

### Email Marketing

At **8.68 / 8.9**, this is not a weak course.

It is the strongest channel-specific email route in the pool, but the core index does not need every high-quality channel certification. Its incremental value is too small once a broad digital-marketing incumbent is already published.

### Social Media Marketing

The course has unusually dense quiz practice, but live-platform terminology and mechanics age quickly. Its role overlaps materially with the broader canonical Digital Marketing course.

### Digital Advertising

Paid-media specialisation is useful, but the course scores lower on Currency and Depth and relies on a rapidly changing platform environment. Current platform documentation remains a better supplement than another core HubSpot slot.

### Inbound Sales

This is the only dedicated sales survivor, but Phase 4 is not a quota-filling exercise.

At 3h06 and **8.36 / 8.6**, it does not clear a high enough comparative bar simply because dedicated sales coverage is otherwise absent. The project should prefer a temporary gap to publishing a merely good course as a global best-in-class choice.
