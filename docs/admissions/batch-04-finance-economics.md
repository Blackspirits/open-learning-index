## Editorial decisions

### Scores are not admission thresholds

The clearest case is `mit-15-401-finance-theory-i`: its **9.04 Quality** is higher than several admitted courses, but Finance & Economics is currency-sensitive. A 2008 graduate finance course is still excellent for valuation, derivatives and portfolio theory, yet it should not be elevated as a current core route without modern supplementation.

Conversely, `saylor-bus103-financial-accounting` is admitted at **8.76 Quality** because it fills a foundational accounting need better than the surviving alternative and has explicit September 2025 maintenance.

### General economics redundancy

The index does not need two broad principles courses for each of microeconomics and macroeconomics.

MRU wins both local comparisons because its much denser quiz and interactive structure produces greater independent-learning value. Saylor remains a strong alternative, especially for learners who value explicit 2025 syllabus maintenance and free certification, but that is insufficient marginal value for duplicate canonical slots.

### Provider concentration

Three MRU courses are admitted. This is a material concentration, but it is justified by three genuinely different needs:

1. general microeconomics;
2. general macroeconomics;
3. development economics.

The project is not mirroring the MRU catalogue: other MRU candidates were already rejected earlier, and each admitted course wins a distinct comparison family.

MIT receives two additional admissions despite already being heavily represented globally. Both clear a higher marginal-value bar because they are unusually current (Fall 2025 and Spring 2026), rigorous and role-distinct.

### Practical finance versus finance theory

`khan-financial-literacy` is admitted as the practical personal-finance route because it was substantially refreshed in January 2026 and combines broad real-world coverage with mastery assessment. It does **not** replace graduate finance theory; it fills a different learner need.

The lack of a current broad free finance-theory winner is left as an honest coverage limitation rather than solved by promoting the older MIT 15.401 archive.
