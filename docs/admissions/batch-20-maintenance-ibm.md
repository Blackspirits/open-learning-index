## Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals

The restored IBM route is valid:

- free credential;
- graded quizzes and final assessments;
- practice simulations;
- approximately ten hours;
- broad coverage of AI, machine learning, neural networks, language, vision and ethics.

That is enough for a strong Deep Review score, but not enough for another canonical general-AI slot.

The incumbent family is unusually strong:

- `google-ml-crash-course` — **9.44 / 9.5**: highly current and interactive;
- `kaggle-intro-machine-learning` — **9.02 / 9.2**: exceptionally low-friction notebook practice plus free credential;
- `harvard-cs50-ai` — **9.62 / 9.7**: deep project-heavy AI foundations.

IBM does not displace any of these and does not add a sufficiently distinct learning need.

Decision: **do_not_admit**.

## Data Fundamentals

IBM's seven-hour route is also legitimate:

- free digital credential;
- data concepts and methodology;
- cleaning/refining;
- visualisation;
- graded assessment;
- practice simulations.

The direct incumbent, `cisco-data-analytics-essentials`, remains decisively stronger for the canonical role:

- approximately 30 hours;
- spreadsheets;
- SQL;
- Tableau;
- more than twenty labs;
- module exams;
- final exam;
- portfolio-oriented analysis.

IBM is a useful concise alternative, but a selective index should not duplicate a role with a substantially narrower course simply because both are good.

Decision: **do_not_admit**.
