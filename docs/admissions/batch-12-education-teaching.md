## Editorial principles

### Teacher development is not one learning need

The three admissions cover different roles:

- broad online/blended teaching practice;
- direct classroom resource adaptation;
- teacher-educator development and communities of practice.

This is why two British Council courses can coexist without admitting the whole provider catalogue.

### Provider concentration matters most among adjacent CPD modules

Four British Council survivors are all valid professional-development courses.

Phase 4 retains only:
- the strongest broadly transferable classroom-methodology option;
- the distinct teacher-educator route.

Communicative Tasks and Understanding Language Systems remain useful specialist reviews without becoming separate core slots.

### Stable pedagogy can survive age, but digital-teaching context still moves

OpenLearn Teaching and learning tricky topics remains pedagogically sound, but a 2019 evidence base matters when digital tools, learning environments and supporting research have changed.

Take your teaching online is itself not perfectly current (last formally updated in 2022), so it receives a 180-day review cadence rather than being treated as timeless.
