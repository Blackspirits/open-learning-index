## Editorial principles

### Original classroom quality is not current independent-study quality

MIT 15.279 remains the highest intrinsic-quality course in the family, but the open archive cannot reproduce much of the original learning loop:

- peer/instructor feedback;
- team assignments;
- live Q&A;
- recorded presentations and self-review;
- interactive classroom exercises.

Phase 4 therefore does not promote it despite **9.08 Quality**.

### Broad communication and specialist communication can coexist

OpenLearn Effective communication in the workplace becomes the general route.

Saylor presentations and crisis communication are retained separately because they serve narrower competencies that require deeper treatment and, especially for crisis communication, stronger currentness.

### Different writing modes justify separate routes

- creative writing → production, character, setting, drafting;
- academic writing → assignments, research, essay/report structure, revision;
- workplace communication → email/report writing inside a broader interpersonal/professional context.

These are not treated as interchangeable simply because all involve writing.

### Certificates do not substitute for a portfolio

Saylor PRDV002 remains useful, but auto-graded assessment and a final exam are not equivalent to a substantial writing portfolio or feedback loop. Its content overlaps enough with the selected workplace route that a separate core slot is unnecessary.

## Feedback limitation

The admitted set still cannot fully solve a structural problem in free independent writing/speaking education: **authentic personalised critique** is scarce.

The index records this honestly rather than inflating auto-graded assessment. This is a quality limitation to monitor, not by itself a reason to restart broad discovery.
