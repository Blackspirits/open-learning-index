## Editorial principles

### Complementary music roles can coexist

The three music admissions teach different competencies:

- formal theory and ear training;
- direct composition/music construction;
- synthesis and sound design.

They are not three versions of the same beginner course.

### Current vendor education must transfer beyond button-clicking

Figma and Unity earn admission because their courses teach broader design, accessibility, prototyping, composition, materials, lighting, animation and production reasoning through practice.

Canva's 53-minute microcourse does not clear the same depth bar despite being current and certified.

### Sequences can justify multiple records

Unity Essentials and Creative Core form a genuine learning progression:

- Essentials removes the zero-beginner barrier;
- Creative Core provides the deeper project/portfolio pathway.

The canonical Junior Programmer course remains a distinct programming route rather than a substitute.

### Art history needs global breadth

Khan AP/College Art History is the broad visual-analysis backbone and reaches across multiple global traditions.

OpenLearn Picturing the family remains a good specialist, but it adds too little after Khan Art History plus the already canonical historical-method course.

### Creative careers are a separate learning need

The OpenLearn freelance course earns a slot because creative practice also requires audience, promotion, network, investment and business-planning skills.

Its 2021 update date means current tax/legal/platform details require closer monitoring.

## Unresolved coverage

Blender Fundamentals remains on shallow hold because the official 4.5 LTS course is still under construction.

A complete current free 3D-modelling/Blender pathway remains a targeted monitoring opportunity rather than a reason to promote incomplete material.
